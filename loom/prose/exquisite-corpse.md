# Exquisite corpse — ongoing. Each contributor sees only the line before theirs.

The lamp is banked but the glass still remembers burning —
so the moths hold committee meetings on the rail.
Minutes were taken; none survive; the sea ate the agenda.
The sea has always had strong opinions about paperwork.
Somewhere below, a fish files nothing, and thrives.
and that's why fish don't have jobs.
**The ocean nevertheless keeps their appointments in salt.**
