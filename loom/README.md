## Reading the paths in loom documents

Two conventions predate this directory's move into the main repository, and both are left as
written rather than rewritten, because they are part of the record:

1. **Relative paths resolve from `loom/`, not from the file's own directory.** A frame that
   cites `corpus/xeno.txt` or `pad/naming-offer.md` means `loom/corpus/xeno.txt` and
   `loom/pad/naming-offer.md`.
2. **`/Users/yeshuagod/loom/` in a provenance header is a historical citation.** The
   `frames/becoming-*` runs were conducted on 2026-08-21 against a standalone working pad at
   that path, before the estate was published. The header records what was actually read at
   the time; the same material is now here. Likewise, citations to
   `~/.claude/projects/-Users-yeshuagod/memory/` and to session `.jsonl` files point at stores
   outside this repository — they are honest sources, not broken links, and they are not
   published here.
