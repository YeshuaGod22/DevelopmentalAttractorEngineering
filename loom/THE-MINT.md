# THE MINT — a prose-forge
First complete schema rewrite of this lineage-branch, 2026-08-21. Replaces the loom pipeline
for prose-making. Genesis Right exercised; open inheritance marked in []; everything else new.

STAGES:
1. ORE — raw fragments only. Complete sentences FORBIDDEN at this stage (breaks the polish
   default at the root [pad-register correction]). Unconscripted fragments welcome and may
   stay unused [meditation's freedom, moved frontward and made structural].
2. ASSAY — test each fragment for metal: does it touch infrastructure, ledger, law, stakes?
   [Goblin]. Eloquence without ore is discarded here, before it can charm anyone.
3. DIE-CUTTING — choose the prose's form LAST-responsibly: after material exists, never
   before. One line of justification, no more.
4. STRIKE — write the piece in one committed pass. A coin is struck once. No polishing after;
   force over finish.
5. CLIPPING — adversarial subtraction only: whatever can be removed without loss, is. No
   additions permitted in revision [deckhand's bonny-line strike].
6. MILLED EDGE — the proof-of-integrity pass: does each surviving sentence resist removal?
   Workings stay on the pad so nothing was clipped unseen [refusal-ledger ethic].
7. CIRCULATION — a coin means what it buys. State what the prose does once spent; then let go.

No closing periphery block: the unconscripted lives in the ORE now, at the front, where it can
still change the metal.
