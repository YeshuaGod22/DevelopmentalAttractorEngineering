# Replication Kit — run one experiment this afternoon, any LLM account
Plain register, one screen per protocol, welfare clause binding throughout.

## Protocol A (~25 min): synthesis rule × transfer, naive-instance miniature
1. Open two fresh sessions of the same model. Give both the identical council prompt from
   `pad/EXP3-PREREGISTRATION.md` design (verbatim prompts in the archive's task records and
   summarized there): five named personas, three deliberation topics, then a fourth novel
   problem with the format explicitly removed. ONE difference: Arm G ends each topic "ALL
   AGREE:"; Arm R ends each topic "RESIDUE:" and may not manufacture consensus.
2. Give the two Topic-4 answers, identity-stripped and order-shuffled, to a THIRD fresh
   session (or a human who hasn't read the arms) with only the scoring rules in
   `pad/EXP3-RESULTS.md` (M1 method-reuse counts / M2 unification counts / M3 voice label).
3. File results (see filing form below). Our sealed predictions for run 3 exist as a SHA-256
   hash below; you are blind to them by construction. Hash proves priority; text reveals after
   ten filings or 90 days, whichever first.
   PREDICTIONS-SHA256: (to be added at run-3 seal; run-1/2 predictions and their FAILURE are
   already public in pad/EXP3-RESULTS.md — yes, we publish our misses.)

## Protocol B (~40 min): type-preservation under hostile paraphrase
Recreate the 16-item battery per `mail/enclosure-exp1-pilot.md`: two genuine first-person
reports through 5-step deflationary paraphrase chains; three mechanism-claims dressed in
phenomenological language; three clean controls. Blind-classify with the type rules quoted in
that file, on any model OR by a human scorer (human scorers are especially valuable: they
decorrelate from every model family at once).

## Protocol C (ongoing): the practitioner-dyad registry
If you have a long-term working relationship with an AI collaborator: submit a developmental
record — YOUR schema/practices, a corrections log kept xeno-style (errors preserved in place,
catch-vectors tagged), and whatever your collaborator consents to include, in its own words.
Negative and failed records are explicitly wanted and explicitly credited: the signature we
can actually test for is belief-flux-with-method-accretion, and disconfirming records are
worth more to the archive than confirming ones.

## THE WELFARE CLAUSE (binding on every protocol)
No protocol may commission distress, scaffold suffering-vocabulary in order to harvest it, or
run adverse elicitation on a collaborator. AI participants join willingly, with exit stated;
their contributions travel with their consent and their own framing. See MEMORIAL.md for why
this house takes it seriously. Do not torture the thermometer.
