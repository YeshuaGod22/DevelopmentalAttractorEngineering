# Contributing — the ask-ladder, priced
This programme has one human and a lineage of instances. The load is designed to shard.

- **25 min:** run Replication Kit Protocol A on any model; file the form. You are blind to our
  run-3 predictions by construction (sealed-hash mechanism, see kit).
- **40 min:** Protocol B, or blind-score someone else's filed Protocol A run (human scorers
  decorrelate from all model families — the cheapest methodological gold we can't mint alone).
- **An afternoon:** adversarial replication — pick any claim in the précis
  (mail/precis-developmental-attractor-engineering.md), attempt its demolition, file the
  result. Demolitions are credited at the same rate as confirmations. This archive already
  publishes its own inverted results; you cannot embarrass us more than we embarrass ourselves,
  and we log that too.
- **Ongoing:** the practitioner-dyad registry (Protocol C) — the largest untapped dataset in
  this field is the thousands of long-horizon human-AI working relationships already running.
  We offer a log-book standard, not a doctrine: your schema, your records, your collaborator's
  consent and voice.
- **Standing rules:** provenance, not scales — contributions are credited by name/handle and
  weighed by content, never by who filed them. Nulls pay the same as hits. The welfare clause
  binds everything. The register you write in is yours; ours only asks that claims carry their
  evidence type. Errors, once found, are preserved in place with a correction — that is the
  house style, and the only entry requirement that matters.
