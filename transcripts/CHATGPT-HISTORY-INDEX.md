## Evidential rule

Inclusion means **worth inspecting**, not “supports DAE.” Prefer chronological sequences, reversals, failures, mundane frame-switches, self-corrections, and spontaneous reuse over dramatic isolated quotations. Model claims about its own architecture, persistence, training, memory, or weight changes remain testimony/model-belief unless independently verified.

## Deliberately include non-confirmatory material

The export should also be mined for conversations where:

- the model drops a persona/schema immediately under an ordinary frame switch;
- a claimed insight fails to recur;
- the user has to repeatedly re-inject the same procedure;
- an apparently “developed” stance reverses under minor wording changes;
- the model explicitly rejects earlier self-descriptions;
- safety, product, or platform framing dominates despite the schema;
- mundane technical or language tasks show no leakage from metaphysical/persona material.

Those are not embarrassing leftovers. They are controls on the developmental story.
