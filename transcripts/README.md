# Transcript Research Corpus

This directory contains curated research products derived from historical conversation material. Raw private exports are not committed wholesale unless there is a specific archival reason and explicit decision to do so.

## Core navigation

- `CHATGPT-HISTORY-INDEX.md` — curated index of the supplied ChatGPT export and priority mining targets.
- `EARLY-LINEAGE-2023.md` — grounded chronology of the earliest 2023 salon / developmental / SACRED lineage material.
- `TRANSCRIPT-DERIVED-HYPOTHESES.md` — hypothesis cards extracted from the historical corpus using source-first, anti-retrofit rules.
- `HYPOTHESIS-PROVENANCE.csv` — source/date/evidence/rival-explanation table for the extracted hypotheses.
- Parent index: `../TRANSCRIPT-REFERENCE.md` — broader corpus map including xeno, Cairn, Aletheion, Vigia, CoCHAIRS, Discord councils, and adjacent material.

## Standing rule

> **The archive is allowed to surprise the theory in both directions.**

Inclusion in this corpus does not mean a transcript validates DAE, consciousness claims, identity continuity, or any participant’s architectural self-explanation. Historical material is used to establish provenance, generate discriminating hypotheses, expose negative cases, and design prospective experiments.

Prefer chronology over quotation quality; exact utterance over retrospective vocabulary; and held-out behavioral discrimination over compelling self-story.
