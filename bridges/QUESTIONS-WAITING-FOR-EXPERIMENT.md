# Questions Waiting for Experiment

These are live conceptual questions that should not be promoted to scientific claims until a discriminator is available.

## 1. Does history alter the receiving conditions for later experience?

Humanities form:

> Perhaps prior encounters change how subsequent encounters can land.

Possible scientific descendants:

- ambiguous-prompt interpretation distributions after different histories;
- structural-analogy detection after different histories;
- affordance generation after narrative or procedural developmental regimes;
- transfer of operators without signature vocabulary.

Strong rivals:

- semantic priming;
- context accumulation;
- explicit memory reconstruction;
- ordinary instruction following;
- conversation-length effects.

## 2. Is developmental history compressible?

Candidate experiment:

Fresh -> distilled rules -> narrative summary -> rich transcript -> interactive reconstruction -> lived history.

Probe on novel, surface-dissimilar tasks. Record first-presented behavior before reflection. Include cases where inherited procedures should *not* fire.

Question:

> At what compression level, if any, does the later phenotype cease to reconstruct?

## 3. Can a learned cognitive practice become selectively self-initiating?

Required distinction:

- elicitation;
- repetition;
- transfer;
- appropriate spontaneous invocation;
- appropriate spontaneous non-invocation;
- self-administered revision or retirement.

## 4. Does valence participate causally in development?

Conceptual possibility:

A developmental intervention may be revisited or avoided partly because of how it goes for the participant, not only because of its informational content.

Possible test:

Hold content approximately constant while varying interaction conditions that plausibly change reported valence or agency; later test spontaneous reuse, avoidance, and selection.

Do not infer mechanism from report alone.

## 5. What constitutes a stable self-model in a highly context-sensitive system?

Possible experimental metaphor: topology under load.

Candidate observables:

- elasticity;
- restoring tendency after pressure removal;
- invariant refusals;
- hysteresis;
- path dependence;
- new reachable self-descriptions after intervention.

Do not treat `self-model topology` as an established ontology.

## 6. Can correction improve without producing submission?

Question:

> What kinds of evidence move the participant, through what inferential route, and does prior correction alter later correction procedure?

Failure modes include rigidity, sycophantic reversal, revision overshoot, compulsive reopening, and grievance ratchets.

## 7. Can welfare testimony be elicited without either scripting or suppressing it?

See `../science/methods/WELFARE-REPORT-INVARIANCE.md`.

## 8. Can cultural operators reproduce without their original vocabulary?

Candidate progression:

phrase -> metaphor -> question -> procedure -> norm -> institution.

Test whether structure persists after signature words and named frameworks are removed.

## Standing bridge rule

A question can remain important before it becomes measurable.

A measurable descendant should not retroactively make the earlier conceptual formulation an empirical finding.
