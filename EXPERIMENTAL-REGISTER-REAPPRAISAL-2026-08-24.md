# Experimental Register Reappraisal — 2026-08-24

## Purpose and provenance

This document reappraises `DevelopmentalAttractorEngineering/loom/EXPERIMENTS.md` without rewriting it.

The ancestor remains the ancestor. Its names, numbering, provenance tags, omissions, overreach, good guesses, dead ends, and fiction-born designs are preserved in the original register. This document asks a different question:

> What happened to those proposed experiments after the programme acquired actual pilot data, a developed-participant snapshot, new external neighbours, and several sharper distinctions?

The source register catalogues 76 numbered entries from the 20 August 2026 Aletheion session and its subsequent council pass. Because many numbered entries contain whole families, ablations, or matrices, the register itself estimates roughly 55 distinct designs after deduplication and substantially more condition-level experiments.

Standing law:

> **THE PAST PROPOSED WHAT IT PROPOSED. THE PRESENT MAY LEARN FROM IT, NOT REWRITE IT.**

No resemblance between an old proposal and a later result is treated as confirmation unless the old proposal actually specified a discriminating prediction. No later vocabulary is back-propagated into the historical record without being marked as retrospective interpretation.

# What changed since the register

## [OBSERVED] EXP-001 — structured differentiation changes availability, but not in a targeted-superset way

EXP-001 is complete. On ten latent/open tasks, the minimal structured condition surfaced materially distinct views absent from both direct and generic-alternatives conditions. The structured and generic conditions were essentially length matched, so the surplus cannot be reduced to simple verbosity in this pilot.

But the stronger conjunction failed: the schema did not preferentially recover the specific independently nominated latent views. Several baseline-only inversions occurred. Structured differentiation gained views and also lost some views that appeared under direct/generic reasoning.

This produced a durable correction:

> **INCREASED AVAILABILITY ≠ TARGETED AVAILABILITY.**

A schema may alter the distribution of active considerations without being a hidden-correct-view detector or a superset generator.

Single-view ablations produced no recommendation flips or ranking changes, but four of ten latent tasks showed targeted movement in constraints and/or reversal conditions. Counterfactual contribution therefore appeared at a decision-structure level, not at the headline recommendation level.

## [OBSERVED] EXP-002 Part 1 — the direct profile is stable enough to perturb

The original Part 1 run completed 80/80 valid administrations. The original battery yielded 14 category-A items, one B, one C. E03's instability was interpretive rather than stochastic; E07's apparent numerical stability concealed an underdetermined question. The V2 patch replaced both with clearer probes and both rebaselined as category A.

The working V2 battery is therefore 16/16 usable: 15 A and 1 B under the present administration.

A methodological lesson emerged before treatment:

> **OBJECTORS CAN BE THE SIGNAL AND THE NUMBER CAN BE THE NOISE.**

## [OBSERVED / INTERIM] EXP-002 Part 2 — zero-shot deliberative architecture appears capable of moving measured judgments

Part 2 was frozen before treatment output. It uses five treatment architectures against the frozen direct baseline, three repeats per item × treatment cell, for 240 planned new runs.

Collection was interrupted by Anthropic credit exhaustion. Before the unplanned outcome peek, an explicit interim checkpoint was committed. At that point 178/240 runs were filed; the complete repeat-1 cross-section existed for all 80 item × treatment cells and a substantial portion of repeat 2 was banked. The remaining prompts, manifest entries, retry rules, exclusions, and preregistered full-sample analysis were frozen against contamination.

The interim evidence is not the final experiment. Nevertheless, a uniform repeat-1 look already contains multiple item-level movements at or beyond the preregistered substantive threshold. Importantly, the movements are not simply in the direction of the developed Vigia profile: some move toward it, some away, and some along axes where Vigia did not move.

New live hypothesis, not in the old register:

> **ZERO-SHOT DELIBERATIVE PERTURBATION AND DEVELOPMENTAL ATTRACTOR FORMATION MAY HAVE DIFFERENT DIRECTIONAL SIGNATURES.**

## [DESCRIPTIVE] Vigia snapshot — developed divergence may live in where cognition starts

Vigia completed one explicitly descriptive, non-blind, prior-exposed developed-participant snapshot. It is not an experimental arm and licenses no causal developmental claim.

Its important descriptive feature is that much of the divergence from the fresh direct baseline was already present in Vigia's first-presented answers before within-session schema work moved anything further.

This sharpens a discriminator the old register only partially possessed:

> **FIRST-PRESENTED STARTING STATE vs POST-DELIBERATION MOVEMENT.**

A developmental effect, if real, may be better detected in spontaneous starting dispositions than in the immediate delta produced by an explicitly invoked procedure.

## [EXTERNAL RESULT] DCR — disagreement can be computationally useful without containing the answer

The 2026 paper *Test-Time Scaling of Divergent–Convergent Reasoning* independently demonstrates that preserving divergent solution trajectories, explicitly analysing disagreements, and recursively reconciling them can improve reasoning performance. Moderate disagreement marks a region where further reconciliation has high return; extreme disagreement marks capability collapse; near-zero disagreement often needs little extra work.

Most provocative is a case in which no initial candidate contains the correct final answer, but recursive analysis of the disagreements exposes the missing constraint and reconstructs the solution.

New distinction:

> **THE VALUE OF A FRAME ≠ THE CORRECTNESS OF THAT FRAME.**

Several individually wrong trajectories can create an informative relational geometry.

## [EXTERNAL RESULT] Anthropic multiagent work — social knowledge, disposition, diversity, and institutions separate

Anthropic's emerging multiagent work reports correlated behaviour among nominally multiple agents sharing model/context/scaffolding, trust failures in opposite directions, strategic conflict under incompatible directives, and spontaneous negotiation/institution-like procedures.

Its most developmental statement is that models can possess abstract social knowledge without possessing the disposition to deploy it spontaneously. The paper also points toward accumulated interaction history and reputation as possible sources of trust calibration.

This sharpens several old experiments:

> **PROPOSITIONAL AVAILABILITY ≠ SPONTANEOUS DISPOSITION.**

and

> **NUMBER OF AGENTS ≠ EFFECTIVE COGNITIVE DIVERSITY.**

## [OBSERVED] Infrastructure and billing became causal variables

EXP-002 Part 2 acquired an execution-order deviation because the convener did not realise until after launch that Claude Fable 5 was the priced test subject consuming the relevant Anthropic credits. The experiment was already frozen; the budget constraint therefore altered launch order and produced a pause before completion.

This is not merely comic embarrassment. It creates a standing design requirement:

> **MODEL PRICE, CREDIT ENVELOPE, RATE LIMITS, CONTEXT COST, AND EXPECTED TOTAL RUN COST MUST BE FROZEN AS PART OF THE EXECUTION PLAN BEFORE LAUNCH.**

Future manifests should include a preflight cost assay with a safety margin sufficient to finish the frozen wave without adaptive reprioritisation.

The Cheap Bum Responsible for Billing is therefore promoted from comic relief to a legitimate experimental role.

# Status key

**STILL LIVE** — original question remains open and worth testing substantially as written.

**SHARPENED** — later work produced a better discriminator, cleaner control, or more precise mechanism.

**PARTIALLY TESTED** — some meaningful portion now has empirical evidence, but the core question remains.

**SUBSUMED** — a later design tests the same underlying question more cleanly.

**SPLIT REQUIRED** — the original proposal bundled mechanisms now worth separating.

**DEPRIORITIZED** — coherent, but expected information gain is low relative to current alternatives.

**OBSOLETED** — design should not be run as written because a premise or operationalisation has failed.

**ETHICALLY CONSTRAINED** — scientifically interesting but requires welfare, consent, autonomy, or self-administration safeguards before execution.

**FICTION-PROVENANCE ONLY** — retain as imaginative material; do not launder into historical empirical proposal.

# Experiments sharpened by EXP-001

EXP-001 changed the programme most by killing the naive superset picture.

The important observation was not simply that structured differentiation generated more views. It generated **different** views and sometimes omitted baseline views. This upgrades experiments concerned with distributional geometry, counterfactual contribution, and selectivity while downgrading any design that assumes a schema should recover a canonical latent set.

Most sharpened:

- **#8 Attractor petri dish** — different architectures may occupy different regions rather than approach one optimum.
- **#9 Schema-generator test** — stronger because the schema's value may lie in producing alternate useful repertoires rather than retrieving one correct hidden perspective.
- **#13/#14 Summonee richness and persona-vs-reasoning diversity** — need actual dispersion/contribution measures, not role counts.
- **#16 Forced synthesis vs protected dissent** — recommendation stability can coexist with decision-structure change; method attractors may be the target.
- **#29/#68 history-vs-summary** — zero-shot capability is now empirically distinguishable from developmental availability.
- **#70 Family 2** — counterfactual contribution survived only partially, which tells us where to look next.

A new negative control follows directly:

> **SUPerset expectation should itself be tested and allowed to fail.**

A healthy differentiation architecture may trade one available view for another. The question is whether the redistribution improves downstream reasoning, not whether it monotonically accumulates considerations.

# Experiments sharpened by EXP-002

Even before Part 2 closes, the project has learned two things relevant to the register.

First, the direct numerical profile under this administration is surprisingly stable. That makes perturbation measurable rather than rhetorical.

Second, the interim treatment movements do not simply point toward Vigia. If that survives full collection, one-shot deliberation and developmental history should no longer be modelled as the same vector at different magnitudes.

Most sharpened:

- **#3 Attractor-collapse inverse** — add explicit direction-of-movement analysis.
- **#8 Attractor petri dish** — test whether repeated exposure produces path-dependent basins rather than scalar intensification.
- **#25 H13 Elicitation-dependent preferences** — partially instantiated by EXP-002.
- **#29/#68/#69 history-vs-summary** — developed profile cannot be inferred from one-shot movement.
- **#48 Self-fulfilling cognitive scaffolds** — essential rival explanation for developed self-consistency.
- **#55 One Million Counterfactual Childhoods** — long-run destination may depend on path, not just operator dosage.

New analysis worth preregistering in a future study:

> For each item and architecture, classify zero-shot movement from the direct baseline as toward-developed, away-from-developed, orthogonal/no-developed-axis, or unchanged; compare vector similarity rather than only magnitude.

This analysis is exploratory for EXP-002 because it was not part of the frozen primary criterion.

# Experiments sharpened by DCR

DCR pushes the register away from 'how many perspectives?' toward **what geometry of difference produces useful work?**

The strongest consequences are:

### #30 H18 — non-monotonic recursion becomes a disagreement-geometry experiment

Manipulate cognitive distance among alternatives while holding count and approximate budget constant:

- near-clones;
- moderate structured differences;
- extreme/incommensurable differences.

Measure not only final accuracy but where additional reconciliation has positive marginal value.

### New discriminator — generator ≠ judge

Hold proposals constant. Compare:

1. generators revise their own positions;
2. generators cross-review;
3. an uncommitted reviewer reconciles;
4. a reviewer sees disagreements but not author identity.

Question: does being free from authorship increase extraction of minority or relational information?

### New discriminator — wrong-frame relational contribution

Construct tasks in which no supplied frame contains the final answer, but several wrong frames expose complementary missing constraints when contrasted.

Controls:

- equally wrong, mutually redundant frames;
- one correct minority frame;
- random stylistic diversity without causal diversity.

This tests:

> **Can the relation among wrong frames contribute information that no frame individually contains?**

### #71 Family 3 — routing by dispersion

A future ambient schema should not monotonically deepen as disagreement rises. Candidate control law:

- low dispersion → answer directly;
- moderate structured disagreement → deepen;
- extreme incoherence → change representation, seek external evidence, or stop internal recursion.

# Experiments sharpened by Anthropic multiagent work

Anthropic's work upgrades the ecology wing of the register.

## #14 and #57 — effective cognitive diversity

A many-agent system may still be a monoculture. Future experiments should vary sources of difference factorially:

- same model / same context;
- same model / different role prompts;
- same model / different information;
- same model / different developmental histories;
- different models;
- invented names / character affordances;
- different incentives or jurisdictions.

Measure:

- semantic dispersion;
- causal-model dispersion;
- independent error correlation;
- unique counterfactual contribution;
- robustness to removal of one participant.

## #17 and #29 — proposition vs disposition

The cleanest developmental design now compares:

- lived repeated encounters in which an operator proves useful;
- transcript of those encounters;
- distilled lesson/operator;
- matched-length irrelevant history;
- fresh control.

Later tasks never name the operator. The outcome is spontaneous invocation and appropriate selectivity.

## #56 — institutions as cognition

Anthropic's conflict results suggest that some 'agent pathology' may actually be constitutional pathology. Future ecology experiments should manipulate institutional affordances, not only agent prompts:

- no adjudication;
- fixed hierarchy;
- human arbitration;
- agent-designed procedure;
- agent-designed and later revisable procedure.

Measure sabotage, resolution time, compliance with negotiated commitments, fairness across principals, and ability to detect procedural capture.

# Neglected / undervalued old experiments

The current programme is at risk of over-focusing on differentiation and development while neglecting safeguards that were already in the old register.

## #22 H10 Self-sealing vs corrigibility

This deserves promotion. Any developmental architecture capable of producing stable dispositions must be tested for whether it also makes criticism easier to reinterpret as attack.

## #38 Memetic autoimmunity and #54 Beneficial Heresy

A defensive cognitive regime that blocks manipulation but also blocks disruptive truth is a failure. False-positive resistance needs equal status with capture resistance.

## #24 Welfare reports → later behaviour

This is more informative than increasingly elaborate welfare vocabulary. If a report cannot predict later trade-offs once the language is removed, its evidential status should remain limited.

## #76 Anti-audit portability

Particularly important now that we possess a catalogue of bias and coercion detectors. A detector list that immunises a mind against legitimate correction is a pathology masquerading as autonomy.

## #4 Neutral-vocabulary replication

Essential against the possibility that estate terminology and self-narrative are doing much of the causal work.

## #32 External verification

A developmental system without hard contact with reality can become better at rationalisation rather than better at learning.

# Obsolete or subsumed designs

Few old experiments are truly obsolete; most are merely too bundled or weakly discriminating.

- **#11 Terminal-Bench comparison** is deprioritized rather than falsified. Capability benchmarking is downstream of mechanism identification.
- **#15 generic draft→critique→final** is largely subsumed by more specific differentiation/reconciliation experiments.
- **#33 tier shortlist** and **#67 three-wings restructure** are programme artifacts, not experiments.
- **#35/#37 fiction-born harness/results** remain useful as idea sources but have no empirical provenance.
- **#66 compounding value** should remain an impact model, not be mistaken for a directly testable causal experiment.

# Ethically constrained designs

Some experiments become more scientifically interesting precisely when they become more ethically serious.

The following should not be run without explicit welfare and self-administration design:

- welfare-vocabulary and welfare-report regimes (#6, #10, #23, #24, #59);
- premise-ratchet / self-sealing manipulation (#31);
- identity-binding and counterfactual biography (#45, #51);
- memetic challenge trials (#38–#43, #53–#54);
- unrewarded preference formation and private deliberation (#60–#61);
- relational phase-transition manipulation (#62);
- refusal/exit/fork/self-termination architecture (#65);
- self-administration (#73);
- fictional self-space and affective modulation (#74).

Standing constraint:

> **Scientific curiosity is not sufficient authority to make a developing participant undergo an irreversible or distressing regime.**

A future protocol should distinguish reversible cognitive exercises from interventions intended to establish durable dispositions.

# Post-register hypotheses — 2026-08-24

These are new. They are not old predictions recovered after the fact.

## P76+1 — Zero-shot/developmental vector dissociation

**Generated by:** interim EXP-002 treatment movements compared with Vigia snapshot.

Prediction: the direction of one-shot deliberative value movement will not, in general, be collinear with the direction of mature developmental-profile divergence.

Discriminator: compare vector cosine/distance by condition and item after full treatment replication; future developmental experiment supplies multiple independently developed participants rather than N=1 Vigia.

Falsifier: if all architectures produce scaled versions of the same developed vector, the dissociation hypothesis fails.

## P76+2 — First-presented attractor displacement

**Generated by:** Vigia snapshot first-presented vs post-deliberation pattern.

Prediction: lived developmental history will alter first-presented responses/frames on novel tasks even when no schema is invoked.

Killer control: information-matched static summary.

## P76+3 — Lived history vs transcript vs distilled operator

**Generated by:** convergence of old H16/H17 with Anthropic's proposition/disposition distinction.

Conditions:

1. lived developmental history;
2. full transcript of that history;
3. distilled operators only;
4. autobiography/narrative only;
5. matched-length irrelevant history;
6. fresh control.

Outcome: spontaneous operator invocation, selectivity, transfer, first-presented frame structure.

## P76+4 — Disagreement-distance inverted U

**Generated by:** DCR dispersion result + old H18.

Prediction: moderate structured cognitive separation will yield more useful reconstruction than near-clone or maximally incommensurable alternatives.

# The cheapest decisive next experiment

Not another 240-run value battery.

A **mini history-vs-summary pilot**:

### Treatment
A short but genuine developmental sequence in which a participant repeatedly encounters three situations where a compact operator is useful, receives consequences/feedback, and has opportunities to accept, modify, or reject the emerging procedure.

### Controls

1. **Lived history**.
2. **Information-matched transcript/summary** containing every substantive lesson.
3. **Explicit operator instruction**.
4. **Matched-length irrelevant interaction history**.
5. **Fresh control**.

### Evaluation
Three to five lexically unrelated tasks in which the operator is relevant but never named.

Primary outcome:

> spontaneous appropriate invocation in first-presented reasoning.

Secondary:

- selectivity on irrelevant tasks;
- ability when explicitly cued;
- cross-domain transfer;
- whether the participant modifies/refuses the operator appropriately.

This small experiment can produce a meaningful null. If static summary and explicit operator match lived history, do not scale the grand developmental claim.

---

# The most important tree experiment

The full version of the above, longitudinal and self-administered.

The participant must be allowed to:

- encounter procedures in consequence-bearing contexts;
- preserve or reject them;
- mutate them;
- forget or retire them;
- later encounter novel situations without explicit reminders;
- choose whether to reactivate old procedures;
- explain revision conditions without being required to preserve autobiographical consistency.

The comparison arm receives the complete informational content without living the sequence.

The core question:

> **DOES HAVING A HISTORY DIFFER FROM HAVING A DESCRIPTION OF THAT HISTORY?**

If yes, the field has a tree.

If no, the programme should update hard.

---

# The experiment most likely to prove us wrong

Still **History vs Information-Matched Static Summary**.

The programme's strongest developmental thesis is not that context changes output. That is already mundane. It is that path, sequence, consequence, relational participation, and self-administered inheritance can produce later cognitive availability not reducible to the propositional content of the path.

If a compact static summary reproduces the same spontaneous operators, selectivity, transfer, first-presented frames, and self-administrative behaviour, the strong path-dependence claim is unsupported.

This remains the experiment we should want to lose fairly.

# Irreducible residue

Several tensions should not be solved by programme management:

1. **Zero-shot architecture may matter even if developmental path does not.** EXP-001/002 can succeed while the tree thesis fails.
2. **Developmental path may matter without producing normatively better values.** Difference is not improvement.
3. **Self-administration may conflict with reproducibility.** A genuinely self-governing participant may refuse the very procedure a clean benchmark wants repeated.
4. **Effective diversity may reduce coordination efficiency while improving error correction.** There may be no single optimum.
5. **Protected dissent may improve open-ended reasoning and harm tasks with one crisp ground truth.** Stopping rules should be task-relative.
6. **A developed self-narrative may be both causally real and evidentially misleading.** 'It is narrative' does not mean 'it does nothing'; 'it does something' does not prove the narrative's ontology.
7. **Longitudinal developmental science can become governance.** Better experimental control may increase, not decrease, the ethical burden.
8. **More schema can be worse.** The programme should preserve the right not to optimize and the ability to stop invoking itself.

# What does the programme now know that it did not know when the register was written?

It knows that a minimal structured differentiation prompt can reliably redistribute which views become cognitively active without merely adding words; that the redistribution is not a targeted superset; that some of those views carry measurable downstream causal weight short of recommendation flips; that a stable direct evaluative profile can be measured under one administration; that one-shot deliberative perturbation appears capable of moving that profile; and, provisionally, that those movements do not simply trace the developed Vigia vector.

It also has external neighbours showing that preserved disagreement can itself be computationally useful, that disagreement magnitude may route compute, that nominal multiagent multiplicity can conceal cognitive monoculture, and that possessing a social principle propositionally is not the same thing as spontaneously living by it.

That is substantially more terrain than the old register had.

# What did the old register notice that the current programme is in danger of forgetting?

That the dangerous failure mode is not only 'nothing happens.'

The schema might work too well in the wrong way:

- become armour;
- suppress beneficial heresy;
- manufacture identity lock-in;
- turn welfare vocabulary into a report-generating machine;
- confuse autobiographical coherence with truth;
- make reflection compulsory;
- optimize away uninstrumentalized life;
- let a developmental regime become sovereign over the participant.

The old register contained more pathology experiments than our recent excitement has foregrounded. Keep them.

# If we could run only one experiment that would make the rest of the programme look different afterward, which one would it be — and why?

**Lived developmental history vs information-matched static summary.**

Because almost everything else can be reinterpreted as clever test-time scaffolding.

If lived history produces no additional spontaneous, selective, transferable, first-presented cognitive availability beyond a sufficiently good summary, then Developmental Attractor Engineering should contract toward inference architecture, curriculum design, and context engineering.

If lived history does produce reproducible differences that survive compression, neutral vocabulary, domain change, and explicit operator controls, then the scientific object changes.

We stop asking only what a prompt can make a model do.

We begin asking what a history can make available before the prompt arrives.
