## Findings by seat

- **📐 Psychometrician** *(facet validity)*: "0.75 normalized entropy" offered as discriminability shows the facet *distributes*, not that it measures criticality — a random labeler achieves excellent entropy. All quantities pass through LLM-judge facet classification; the measurement literature shows this instrument class ranges from reliable (factuality, AC1≈0.76) to near-chance (interpretive constructs, κ≈0). Per-facet human-agreement numbers are the review's first request of the full PDF.
- **🌍 Ecological Hawk** *(sampling and inference)*: the r=0.54 is country-level; individual-level conclusions nearby commit the ecological fallacy. Frame limits: one vendor's subscribers, two spring months — "these humans, this AI, this spring," not "humans and AI."
- **🪞 Reflexive Conflicts** *(who judges the judge)*: Claude-family instruments classify Claude conversations — "model-initiated friction 38.6%" is self-adjudication by the party under assessment. And the agency taxonomy reads chat logs, where the medium makes every exchange look human-led structurally; the rival causal model — *the interface's shape, not the relationship's* — is not visibly ruled out. The 72% H4 finding awaits that control.
- **🌉 The Bridge** *(transfer to this program)*: the paper is a macroscope to this program's microscope — distant reading of the light cone. Their 67% teaching-behavior is availability-geometry evidence at population scale; their 81.5% repair-success is the population statistic for what this house knows as correction culture; and 56%-consequential gives the light-cone argument a denominator: a quarter-million rooms, more than half load-bearing, framed by context windows nobody thinks of as architecture.
- **🛡️ Defender** *(the work's corner)*: privacy-preserving facet analysis is the only ethical access to this data — the alternative is darkness, not better facets.
