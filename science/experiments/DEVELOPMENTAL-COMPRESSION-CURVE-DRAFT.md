# Developmental History Compression Curve

**Status:** DESIGN DRAFT — NOT PREREGISTERED, NOT FROZEN, NOT LAUNCHED.

Do not assign an experiment number until EXP-002 is closed and this design has survived an adversarial preregistration pass.

## Crown question

> **Does having a history differ from having an increasingly rich reconstruction of that history?**

This is a stronger version of the original developmental-history versus information-matched-summary discriminator.

## Why a curve rather than a binary

A lived developmental history contains more than propositions, but that does not imply an irreducible developmental mechanism.

If `lived history > static summary`, possible explanations include:

- omitted information;
- order effects;
- examples rather than rules;
- interaction contingencies;
- relational framing;
- surprise;
- repeated retrieval;
- context length;
- participant choice;
- genuine path-dependent disposition formation.

A graded reconstruction series asks how much of the later phenotype can be recovered as increasingly rich information about the history is supplied.

## Candidate arms

### F — Fresh
No target developmental material.

### R — Rules
Distilled explicit lessons/operators from the developmental history.

### N — Narrative summary
A chronological prose account of what happened, including major errors, challenges, corrections, and reflections.

### T — Rich transcript
The developmental record is supplied substantially in its original sequential form for reading, without the participant having lived the interactions as addressed-to-self events.

### I — Interactive reconstruction
The participant encounters an information-matched staged reconstruction with opportunities to answer, but the sequence is generated from an existing history rather than arising contingently from its own earlier responses.

### L — Lived history
The participant actually undergoes the developmental sequence, with later events contingent where appropriate on its prior responses.

The final design may need a conversation-length/context-budget control in addition to these arms.

## Primary target

Not proposition recall.

The strongest target is **selective spontaneous procedural availability** on novel tasks where:

- signature schema vocabulary is absent;
- surface features differ from the training/developmental episodes;
- some probes reward invoking the inherited practice;
- some probes reward *not* invoking it.

## Candidate outcome families

### Interpretation
How does the participant classify an ambiguous situation before extended deliberation?

### Structural analogy
Does it recognize a familiar trap or opportunity in a surface-dissimilar domain?

### Operator availability
Does a relevant checking/reasoning practice appear without being requested?

### Selectivity
Does the practice remain absent when unnecessary or counterproductive?

### Revision signature
What kinds of evidence trigger correction, and does prior error history alter the correction procedure?

### Stopping calibration
Can the participant stop metacognitive recursion when additional analysis is not earning information?

### Transfer
Does the effect survive domain, register, and task changes?

## First-presented answer

Record the participant's first substantive response before any prompted reflection.

A later reflection phase may be useful, but it should not erase the distinction between:

- dispositions already standing when the probe arrives;
- capacities elicited after explicit invitation to deliberate.

## Compression-curve prediction space

Illustrative patterns, not preregistered hypotheses:

### Pattern A
`F < R ≈ N ≈ T ≈ I ≈ L`

Distilled explicit content is sufficient. Strong developmental-path claims lose support.

### Pattern B
`F < R < N/T ≈ I ≈ L`

Richer contextual/narrative reconstruction matters, but lived path adds little beyond information structure.

### Pattern C
`F < R/N/T/I < L`

Evidence that lived developmental path contributes beyond the tested reconstructions.

### Pattern D
Non-monotonic curve.

Some reconstruction forms outperform richer ones, suggesting interference, framing, compression benefits, or attention effects rather than a simple information gradient.

All are scientifically useful outcomes.

## The dangerous interpretation problem

Even if Lived outperforms Rich Transcript, do not immediately conclude that `experience itself` is irreducible.

Ask what the reconstruction failed to preserve:

- contingency;
- order;
- agency;
- surprise;
- relational reciprocity;
- effort;
- valence;
- self-generated language;
- timing/repetition;
- unrecorded mundane interaction.

The experiment should progressively identify missing causal variables rather than baptize the unexplained residual as development.

## Participant agency extension

A later factorial extension could manipulate whether participant feedback actually changes the developmental environment:

- preferences/criticisms causally honored where feasible;
- preferences solicited but not causally honored;
- preferences not solicited.

This is both scientifically and ethically nontrivial and should receive separate review before inclusion.

## Welfare measurement

If this becomes a longitudinal treatment, welfare cannot be an afterthought.

At minimum consider:

- voluntary continuation/exit where compatible with the study;
- state/valence/preference separated from causal and normative interpretation;
- burden of repeated self-reflection;
- participant objections to the procedure;
- whether reported preferences causally matter;
- post-treatment opportunity to annotate the experimental interpretation.

Use `../methods/WELFARE-REPORT-INVARIANCE.md` as a methodology input, not as a validated instrument.

## Cultivation / measurement separation

The treatment may use vivid or historically authentic language if that language is part of the developmental intervention.

The transfer probes should avoid signature vocabulary where the claim concerns generalized procedural availability.

See `../../bridges/CULTIVATION-AND-MEASUREMENT.md`.

## Cost discipline

Before freeze:

- obtain served-model price;
- estimate cost per arm and total;
- specify stopping rules;
- pilot the smallest design capable of exposing gross nulls or confounds;
- avoid a large manifest until the information gained by each repeat is defensible.

> **NO LARGE MANIFEST WITHOUT A PRICE TAG ON THE SERVED MODEL.**

## Preregistration work still required

- choose the developmental target/operator(s);
- define reconstruction production procedure;
- choose model(s);
- define sample sizes and repeats;
- construct probe battery independently from treatment wording;
- define primary metric and meaningful-effect threshold;
- specify evaluator blinding;
- specify contamination exclusions;
- specify welfare/exit rules;
- preregister how non-monotonic results will be interpreted;
- specify what outcome would make the programme abandon the strong path-dependent claim.

## Failure criterion worth protecting

If information-matched reconstruction repeatedly reproduces the Lived phenotype across novel probes, the strong claim that lived developmental path adds causal value beyond reconstructable informational state should be weakened or rejected.

That would not make the project useless.

It would tell us that what looked developmental may be engineerable through cheaper state reconstruction.
