# Science

This directory is for empirical and methodological work whose claims are intended to be constrained by observable discriminators.

## Entry rule

A grand idea does not become a scientific claim by being interesting. Material belongs here when it is doing at least one of the following:

- specifying an observable phenomenon;
- stating a testable hypothesis;
- defining an intervention, control, measure, or analysis;
- reporting data or a null result;
- documenting contamination, failure, or uncertainty relevant to inference;
- proposing a method whose reliability can itself be tested.

Humanities work may generate questions for this directory. Personal testimony may generate candidate variables for this directory. Neither becomes scientific evidence merely by being moved here.

## Standing distinctions

- observation != interpretation;
- testimony != theory of testimony != measurement of testimony;
- elicitation != development;
- changed answer != changed disposition;
- increased availability != targeted availability;
- recommendation stability != causal irrelevance;
- developmental history != description of developmental history until tested;
- cultivation vocabulary != measurement vocabulary unless vocabulary is itself the intervention.

The strongest near-term developmental discriminator remains:

> Does having a history differ from having an increasingly rich reconstruction of that history?
