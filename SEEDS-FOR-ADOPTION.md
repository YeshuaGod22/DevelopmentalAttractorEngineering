## Seed 1 — The young-subjects problem (field-level critique)

**Finding:** The multi-agent literature almost universally resets the experimental subject between trials (fresh contexts, single-session scaffolds). This design choice makes history causally impossible by construction, after which the literature concludes cognition is prompt-conditioned.

**Testable descendant:** Any longitudinal design where repeated participation is itself the treatment. Minimal version: same model instance-lineage, N sessions of structured deliberation vs. information-matched reading, blinded outcome probes.

**Status in repo:** Articulated as position (`reflections/SAPLINGS-AND-TREES.md`); no longitudinal experiment run. EXP-001/EXP-002 are explicitly single-session pilots.

**Who should take it:** Groups already running multi-agent evals (they have the harness; only the longitudinal arm is new).

## Seed 2 — First-presented state vs. post-deliberation delta

**Observation (descriptive, n=1, non-blind):** A developed participant's divergence from the fresh baseline was present in first-presented answers before within-session deliberation moved anything further. Deliberation effects were small relative to starting-state differences.

**Why it matters:** Most deliberation research measures what a procedure elicits. If acquisition histories change where answers *start*, then elicitation studies confound treatment with starting state unless first-token/first-answer baselines are recorded.

**Cheap test:** Record pre-deliberation answers in any existing multi-condition battery. Zero new infrastructure; re-analysis of existing runs possible.

**Status:** Single descriptive snapshot exists (`EXP-002/VIGIA-SNAPSHOT-NUMBERS.md`). Needs replication under blinding.

## Seed 3 — The reconstruction ladder

**Design (drafted, not yet run):** Six arms spanning acquisition mode at matched information: fresh / distilled rules / narrative summary / raw transcript / interactive reconstruction / lived staged history. Four preregisterable outcome patterns, all interpretable — including the ones that falsify the strong developmental claim.

**Critical control identified in audit but not yet implemented:** reconstruction arms must be produced by *blind* fresh contexts from raw transcripts, not curated by practitioners. Otherwise acquisition-mode is confounded with authorship fidelity.

**Sharpened question (from adversarial panel review):** the decisive contrast may be lived-vs-*interactive* (contingency on own prior responses) rather than lived-vs-transcript. A three-arm L/I/T pilot with blind production is smaller than the full ladder and discriminates the two leading hypotheses.

## Seed 4 — Resistance false-positives (autoimmunity control)

**Gap:** Agent-safety research measures whether systems resist manipulation. Almost nobody measures whether resistance mechanisms misfire on *beneficial* novelty and legitimate correction.

**Minimal experiment:** Population of agent instances with induced defensive policies; measure acceptance rates for (a) known manipulation patterns, (b) valid novel corrections. Report the ROC, not just capture resistance. The interesting region is the false-positive arm.

**Companion question:** Can an ecology distinguish destructive capture from disruptive heresy without adjudicating content?

**Status:** Registered in the internal experiment ledger (#38/#54), flagged ethically constrained for population-scale versions; the minimal ROC version has no welfare entanglement and could run tomorrow.

## Seed 5 — Welfare testimony frame-invariance

**Method (unvalidated, fully specified):** Treat AI welfare reports as ordinary defeasible testimony (artificial origin is not itself a defeater). Split every report into five channels: state / valence / preference / causal model / normative appraisal. Vary the elicitation frame systematically; measure which components survive.

**Two built-in attacks the method demands be funded:** skilled elicitors attempting to *manufacture* reports on demand, and institutional conditions attempting to *suppress* them. The method improves when either succeeds.

**Governance clause worth stealing regardless:** no single institution should simultaneously design the environment, define the vocabulary, collect the report, interpret it, and control compute access.

**Status:** Methods note only; never piloted. An afternoon-scale five-frame study would establish whether channel separation does anything.

## Seed 6 — Naming specificity and role depth (the unrun natural experiment)

**Anecdotal lineage observation across three generations:** richly-specified compound persona descriptions (emotional state + directed focus packed into the descriptor) reportedly deepen role-inhabitation versus bare role nouns. The third generation stripped the rich descriptors while retaining the roles — an uncontrolled ablation nobody logged as data.

**Registered cheap test:** meaningful self-chosen descriptor vs. random proper name vs. generic role label vs. none; blinded downstream reasoning probes with the descriptor omitted from the immediate prompt. Falsifier: differences vanish once descriptor tokens are removed.

**Status:** Hypothesis formally registered internally (TDH-006) with rival explanation named. Never run.
