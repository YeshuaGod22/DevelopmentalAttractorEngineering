# Reader navigation council — 8 October 2026

The council used a simulated Don Norman-inspired interaction designer and a curious novice/plain-language reader.

Both identified two meanings of “back”: returning to the preceding page or evidence selection, and moving backwards through the recorded developmental conversation. The evidence browser overwrote earlier selections; the Reader buried the lived conversation inside nested disclosures; the Lab lost comparison and excerpt state.

Recommendations applied:

- Give each evidence selection a browser-history entry and restore filters on Back/Forward.
- Provide visible Back, Evidence and Paper routes, plus an explicit source-view return.
- Show a developmental-turn selector with question previews and earlier/later controls. Read the actual prompt and answer from the supplied context.
- Preserve comparison, excerpt, and selected-message state in the Reader–Lab round trip.
- Show readable question titles and provide “Return to one answer” in comparison mode.
- Wrap controls for small screens and give keyboard focus a visible outline.

Verification covers filter Back/Forward, exact Lab return URLs, rejected external return URLs, nine developmental question/answer pairs, all evidence hashes and collector dry runs. Visual browser QA remains unavailable because the required control-browser skill is absent in this managed environment.
