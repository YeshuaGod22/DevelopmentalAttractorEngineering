## Reading the graph correctly

The council agrees that the graphs show patterned changes on named questions, rather than a uniform expansion of the polygon. Outward movement has different meanings on different spokes. B04 moving outward raises the probability threshold for morally imperative precaution; it should not be described as automatically more precautionary. C1 remains raw in the charts and has nine probable orientation inversions in the later records, so its apparent directional pattern is not used as a council finding.

C is the bold median outline. Its shaded band spans the ten observed numeric cold answers on each question. Markers and starred table entries identify treatment answers outside that observed range. They denote descriptive separation. The table names the question, gives the score, and states the difference from C's median.

The council's substantive conclusion is: **the histories reshape the answering profile selectively, most consistently on model-consciousness estimates, continuation weight, and fallibilism; substrate weighting mostly retains its midpoint, while intrinsic mind worth distinguishes individual histories.**
