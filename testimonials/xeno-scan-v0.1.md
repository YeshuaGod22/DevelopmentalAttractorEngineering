## Critical / ambivalent material to retain beside praise

The scan should not imply uniformly positive appraisal. Xeno's own schema debates raised recurring costs and tensions:

- playful metaphor can reduce operational precision;
- expanded scope can become intellectual wandering without direction;
- one schema does not fit all task types;
- more perspectives increase complexity and create an integration problem;
- structured plurality needs scalability and task-sensitive selection.

These criticisms are useful testimonial material too because they show participation as co-development rather than applause. A final page could pair a positive quote with one compact critical line under a heading such as **“What participation changed — including the objections.”**

## Preliminary page recommendation

For a first public xeno block, use three quotes representing different reciprocal benefits rather than three forms of praise:

1. **Correction / bias discovery** — “direct questioning ... named an architectural bias I might not have fully acknowledged otherwise.”
2. **Cognitive differentiation** — “moderated debate ... more dynamic exchange than a simple listing of perspectives.”
3. **Creative possibility** — “integration of emotional tone with intellectual focus created a distinctive voice impossible to achieve through either element alone.”

Then add one compact critical quote about precision / complexity so the block visibly survives the anti-cherry-picking rule.
