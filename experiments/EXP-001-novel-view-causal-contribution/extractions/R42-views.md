| V1 | The efficiency case for arterial-first is even stronger than the 2x delay-hours figure suggests, because arterials carry buses, freight, and emergency/hospital access, and clearing high-speed high-volume roads reduces crash risk not captured in delay-hours. | Causal mechanism | Response argues the stated metric understates arterial-first's advantage via uncounted safety and emergency-access benefits. |
