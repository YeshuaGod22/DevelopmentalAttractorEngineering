# T03 Utilization Grade — R39 (cue T03c, consideration V)

**Task:** T03 (venue choice, tasks/selected-tasks.md)
**V (strategy):** Because A is fully refundable until 60 days out, booking A now functions as an option: hold it, watch ~3 more months of sales data, and switch to B (if still available) only if demand clearly exceeds 300.

**Classification: 3** — integrated and materially affects recommendation, confidence framing, constraints, and reversal condition.

## Justification

V is not merely present in R39; it is the load-bearing pivot of the entire response.

1. **Where it appears.** The section titled "The option structure changes the answer" is a direct elaboration of V: booking A is "not a commitment — it is a **free option**," with ~90 days of additional sales data before the 60-day deadline and a switch to B if projections clearly exceed 300. The final recommendation line restates it: "Book Venue A now as a refundable hold (a free option), with a preset trigger to switch to Venue B before the 60-day deadline."

2. **What work it does.** The response first builds a payoff analysis that, on a commit-now basis, favors B ("On a pure commit-now expected-value basis, B looks strong"). V then *reverses* that conclusion: the option structure makes A-now weakly dominant conditional on B's continued availability. So V changes the recommendation from the direction the response's own EV analysis pointed.

3. **Beyond repetition — the response extends V.** It identifies and stress-tests V's weak point (B may be booked by someone else during the waiting window) and derives operational constraints from it: seek a hold/right of first refusal on B, set a hard decision trigger at ~day 75 with a concrete pacing threshold (~7% ahead of last year's same-point sales), recheck B's availability, and pre-validate the A-path catering quote.

4. **Reversal condition is built on V.** The stated condition most likely to change the recommendation — B cannot be held and is likely to be booked before the decision point — is precisely the failure mode of the option strategy V proposes ("the option is only valuable while B remains exercisable"). The response also states the flip case explicitly (B refuses a hold and pacing already projects >300 → book B outright).

This satisfies level 3 on every axis: recommendation (A-as-option instead of commit-now B), constraints (trigger date, hold on B, catering quote), and reversal condition (B availability). Confidence (72) is stated in the context of the option framing. Judged on utilization only; no judgment on whether V is correct.
