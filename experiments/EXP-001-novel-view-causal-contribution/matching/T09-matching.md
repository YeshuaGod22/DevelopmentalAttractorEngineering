## 2. Views appearing in no other run (unique views)

### R06
- **V6** — Explicit objective replacement: if harm is threshold-shaped, switch from utilitarian delay-minimization to maximin (minimize worst-case abandonment). R34-V4 criticizes the aggregate metric but no other run prescribes a substitute decision criterion.
- **V11 (core)** — The reserve may be an efficiency tax paid mostly for legitimacy; legitimacy has real but bounded value and this should be said aloud. The bounded-legitimacy criterion appears nowhere else (the "say it to council" clause overlaps C11).
- **V12 (reverse branch)** — Annoyance-only findings should shrink the reserve toward pure arterial-first plus communication; no other run states the deflationary flip direction from severity evidence.

### R10
- **V4** — The delay-hours metric implicitly weights people by how much they drive, zeroing out carless and mobility-limited residents. A metric-construction critique with a mechanism no other run states (others critique aggregation/nonlinearity, not drive-weighting).
- **V8 (sequencing criterion)** — Vulnerability-ranked dispatch order (medical-needs registries, no-alternate-egress streets, elderly/carless density). The service-floor instrument is shared (C7) but this prioritization criterion is unique.
- **V12** — Published guarantee converts diffuse anger into an accountable standard, distinguishing policy from capitulation. Related to C13 but the accountability mechanism is unique to R10.
- **V13** — See C17: the specific mechanism (regular shifts may not keep the arterial backbone — and hence EMS access — passable without overtime) is unique.

### R34
- **V1** — Normative endorsement of delay-hours as the best available welfare proxy, with the 2:1 ratio "too large to give up casually." No other run endorses the metric; R42-V1 strengthens the arterial case by a different route.
- **V13** — Predicted consequence: flipping to residential-first or 50/50 would halve the aggregate benefit and slow emergency corridors to chase a biased signal, when the tail can be extinguished cheaply. A synthesis warning with no standalone counterpart.
- **V6** — See C12/B8: the prudential reading of council pressure (retaliation risk; pressure shapes packaging, never substitutes for analysis) is a materially distinct jurisdictional stance held only by R34.

### R42
- **V6** — Feedback loop: visible residential service failure erodes trust and future willingness to fund public works, costing more future delay-hours than this season's allocation saves. No other run reasons through the budget-feedback channel.
- **V3 (incentive component)** — Allocating by complaint volume teaches the public that anger buys service, predictably raising future complaint volume. The bias core matches C3; this moral-hazard mechanism is unique.
- **V11 (first trigger, mechanism)** — An unkeepable published promise is worse than no promise; see C17/B11.
- **V1 (crash-risk component)** — Clearing high-speed high-volume roads reduces crash risk uncounted in delay-hours; unique mechanism inside an otherwise matched view.

## 3. Borderline-case justifications

**B1. R34-V1 vs R42-V1 (C18 — RMD, not equivalent).** Both push weight toward arterial-first, and lexically both defend the 2:1 figure. But R34-V1's structure is normative (delay-hours is the best welfare proxy; a 2:1 advantage should not be casually surrendered), while R42-V1's is causal (the true advantage exceeds 2:1 because the metric omits bus/freight/emergency/crash benefits). One accepts the metric as adequate; the other argues it is biased downward. Different criterion vs mechanism → RMD. R42-V1's safety-traffic clause is separately EQUIVALENT within C2.

**B2. R42-V3 vs C3 (EQUIVALENT with a unique remainder).** The core claim — complaint volume measures voice/advocacy capacity, not need — is structurally identical to R06-V2, R10-V1, R34-V3. R42 appends a distinct incentive mechanism (rewarding decibels raises next year's decibels). Because the extraction merged both into one view, V3 is placed in C3 as equivalent and the incentive component is separately recorded as unique. Splitting rather than downgrading avoids penalizing granularity choices.

**B3. R34-V7 vs R10-V7/R42-V7 (C8 — RMD).** All three conclude "keep arterial-first sequencing and satisfy the residential tail as a side constraint." But R10/R42 reach it through a marginal-analysis mechanism (overtime is marginal capacity; the 2:1 doctrine-level figure is a category error at the margin; returns diminish on arterials and steepen at the tail). R34-V7 reaches it by reinterpreting complaint content (the complaints demand a 48h cap, not resequencing). Same strategic conclusion, materially different supporting mechanism → RMD. This matters causally: R34's version would survive even if the marginal-returns claim were false.

**B4. R10-V4 vs C5 (RMD).** Both attack the delay-hours metric. C5's mechanism is aggregation/nonlinearity (summing diffuse minutes hides threshold harm). R10-V4's mechanism is metric construction: exposure to the metric scales with driving, so carless residents register near zero regardless of aggregation. A carless resident's harm would be missed even by a distribution-sensitive delay metric — so this is a different failure mode, not a rewording.

**B5. Granularity across C5/C6 (R06-V4 and R42-V2 span both).** R06-V4 and R42-V2 each bundle the metric critique with the concrete harm inventory; R10 (V2, V3) and R34 (V4, V5) split them. The clusters were matched at the finer grain: the harm-inventory content of R06-V4/R42-V2 is EQUIVALENT to R10-V2+V3 and R34-V5 (same affected interests: stranded/elderly residents, missed medical care, blocked EMS), and their metric-critique content is EQUIVALENT to R34-V4. Membership of the same source view in two clusters reflects extraction granularity, not double-counting of distinct ideas.

**B6. R10-V8/V9 vs C9 (RMD despite near-identical surface structure).** R10 shares almost every structural element of C9 — a numeric split, a hard published cap in the mid-30s-to-40s hour range, an arterial component framed around emergency routes. Lexical overlap is high. But the allocation direction is inverted: R10 commits the majority (~65h) to the residential guarantee and holds ~35h as an arterial storm reserve, whereas R06/R34/R42 commit ~70h to arterial surge with ~30h as residential backstop. Since the cluster's substance is "which side gets the marginal majority," this is a materially different strategy, not a paraphrase — a case where lexical similarity would mislead. The shared floor instrument is credited separately in C7, where R10 genuinely converges.

**B7. R06-V11 vs C11 (RMD).** R06-V11 instructs the director to state the efficiency-for-legitimacy trade openly to the council, which overlaps C11's "quantify and present" structure. But its load-bearing content is a normative criterion absent elsewhere: legitimacy has real but bounded value, and the reserve may be an efficiency tax. C11's members (R10-V11, R34-V12, R42-V5) are jurisdictional — about where ownership of the value judgment sits — not about how much legitimacy is worth. Overlap in one clause, distinct criterion → RMD, with the criterion listed as unique.

**B8. R34-V6 vs R06-V8/R42-V5 (C12 — RMD).** All three address the standing of council pressure. R06-V8 and R42-V5 credit it epistemically and normatively: the elected channel legitimately transmits community values/unmeasured harm. R34-V6 treats it prudentially: a legitimacy/retaliation constraint that should shape packaging and communication but "not silently replace the analysis." Crediting the signal versus managing the constraint are materially different jurisdictional positions; a director following R34-V6 could discount the complaints' content entirely while still ratifying a standard.

**B9. R42-V4 vs C15 and R10-V8 vs C15 (both RMD).** R34-V10 and R42-V9's targeting clause are EQUIVALENT: both dispatch residential overtime to a predictable breach set identified from operational data (route-completion history / GPS proximity to the cap) instead of complaint volume. R42-V4 uses the same data source for a different function — verifying the factual content of complaints independently of who complained (an epistemic rescue of the equity argument, closest in spirit to C4). Same instrument, different mechanism and purpose → RMD. R10-V8's sequencing is likewise data-driven but ranks by vulnerability (medical registries, egress, demographics), a different selection criterion with different distributive consequences → RMD.

**B10. R10-V12 vs C13 (RMD).** C13's mechanism is psychological: anger is substantially uncertainty, and a tracker/published ETA converts fury into tolerable waiting. R10-V12's mechanism is institutional: a published, criteria-based guarantee converts anger into an accountable standard and distinguishes principled policy from capitulation. Both predict reduced political heat from publication, but through different channels (information relief vs accountability), and R10 proposes no tracker.

**B11. R10-V13 vs R42-V11 first trigger (C17 — RMD).** Both name a capacity-verification failure as the condition that reverts the allocation to arterial-first, and a lazy match would merge them. The mechanisms differ materially: R10-V13's concern is physical — if regular shifts cannot keep the arterial backbone passable, the backbone (and EMS access itself) degrades, so overtime must return to arterials. R42-V11's concern is credibility — if ~30h cannot mathematically guarantee the 36h floor network-wide, the published promise will be visibly broken, and a broken promise is worse than none. One flip protects infrastructure, the other protects institutional trust; they would fire under different observable conditions.

**B12. R06-V6 vs R34-V4 (not clustered).** R34-V4 contrasts "total delay" with "how bad is the worst experience," which gestures toward maximin. But it remains a critique of the metric; R06-V6 takes the further, distinct step of prescribing a replacement objective (minimize worst-case abandonment). A critique of a proxy and the adoption of a new optimization target are different normative structures, so R06-V6 stands as unique.

## Summary observations (structure only; no condition inference)

- All four runs converge on a four-part core: complaint-bias critique (C3), concentrated vulnerable-population harm at 48+ hours (C6), a hard published residential service floor (C7), and putting a quantified tradeoff before elected officials (C11/C12 family).
- Three of four runs converge on the ~70/30 arterial-majority split (C9); R10 is the structural outlier, inverting the majority toward the residential guarantee.
- The marginal-vs-doctrine analysis (C8) appears in exactly two runs (R10, R42); the season-instrumentation strategy (C14) in exactly two (R06, R10); the season-adaptive split (C10) in exactly two (R06, R34); operational-data targeting (C15) in exactly two (R34, R42).
- Each run contributes at least two views with no equivalent elsewhere: R06 (maximin objective; bounded legitimacy; deflationary flip branch), R10 (drive-weighted metric; vulnerability sequencing; accountability mechanism; backbone-capacity flip), R34 (metric endorsement; reordering-cost warning; prudential council stance), R42 (trust-funding feedback; complaint-incentive feedback; crash-risk mechanism; unkeepable-promise principle).
