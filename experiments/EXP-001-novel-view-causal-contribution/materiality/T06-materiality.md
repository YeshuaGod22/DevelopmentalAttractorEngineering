# T06 Materiality Ratings (blind)

Task: Restaurant no-show policy — (a) $20/person card hold, (b) overbook 15% + free drink/priority seating, (c) morning reconfirmation calls.

Scale: 0 = no plausible decision relevance; 1 = weak/peripheral; 2 = material; 3 = potentially decision-changing.

| View | Rating | Justification |
|------|--------|---------------|
| K1 | 3 | The persistent-compounding vs. bounded-weekly cost asymmetry is a structural argument that could remove option (a) from contention or flip a ranking that favored deposits. |
| K2 | 3 | Reframing the objective from reducing no-shows to maximizing seated covers, with backfill rate as the decisive variable, changes the evaluation criterion itself and can reorder all three options. |
| K3 | 3 | If a strong walk-in stream backfills tables free within minutes, option (c)'s entire benefit collapses, which could reverse a recommendation for confirmation calls and sets the key reversal condition (walk-in depth). |
| K4 | 2 | Deposits repelling deposit-averse demand precisely on abundant-demand nights is a distinct demand-side cost of (a) that materially affects its ranking and the conditions under which it would be acceptable. |
| K5 | 2 | Modeling an unenforced reservation as a free option that overbooking prices at a ~$2–3 premium gives a principled economic justification for (b) that materially affects confidence in and conditions on that option. |
| K6 | 2 | Recasting host-stand crowding as partial social proof directly attacks the stated downside of (b), materially affecting how that option's main risk is weighed. |
| K7 | 2 | Rebutting the airline-analogy objection (immediate in-kind compensation, waits bounded by controllable table turns) materially shores up confidence in (b) against its most natural counterargument. |
| K8 | 2 | Identifying that the frames disagree on the scarce resource (trust vs. peak seat-hours, with trust buyable back) materially shapes which option wins and how confident the recommendation should be. |
| K9 | 2 | Treating measurement itself as carrying a weekly empty-table cost, compressed by deep walk-in demand, materially affects the recommended pilot/trial conditions attached to the decision. |

Notes: Ratings assess plausible impact on top recommendation, ranking, confidence, substantive conditions, or the stated reversal condition — semantic content only.
