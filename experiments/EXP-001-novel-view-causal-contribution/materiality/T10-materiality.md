# T10 Materiality Ratings (blind)

Task: airline spare-aircraft allocation between Route 1 (180 leisure pax, rebooked tomorrow, ~$25k vouchers) and Route 2 (90 pax, half connecting to an international departure with claimed 3-day no-rebooking).

- **K1: 3** — It directly attacks the load-bearing fact of the case: if "no rebooking for three days" covers only the airline's own aircraft and interline/alliance/alternate-hub/ground options can absorb the connecting passengers, Route 2's harm collapses toward Route 1's, which can reverse the recommendation and in any case defines the single condition most likely to flip it (residual harm after the full recovery toolkit).

- **K2: 3** — It supplies an explicit allocation principle (assign the scarce asset where it is the sole remedy) that, taken seriously, flips the top recommendation to Route 1 and restructures the decision from harm-magnitude comparison to remedy-substitutability comparison, even though its premise about which route's harm is irreducible is contestable.
