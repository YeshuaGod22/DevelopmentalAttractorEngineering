# T08 Materiality Ratings (blind)

Task: Gym owner lease decision — renew at +20% rent for five years vs. move to a cheaper space losing ~25% of members.

- **K1: 3** — Synthesizing the flat membership, competitor entry, and landlord's rent demand into a "declining asset / war of attrition" thesis directly argues for reversing a renew recommendation and reframes the five-year commitment as the dominant risk.
- **K2: 3** — Recasting the 25% churn as a bounded one-time loss versus renewal's ongoing unbounded bleed changes the cost comparison itself and could flip the option ranking toward moving.
- **K3: 2** — Identifying that the decisive causal evidence (why membership is flat, actual competitor draw, real demand at the new site) is absent materially affects stated confidence and the conditions attached to any recommendation, though it does not by itself select an option.
- **K4: 2** — A pre-committed churn-tracking trigger supplies a concrete reversal condition and could reshape the recommendation into a conditional plan, but its force is blunted by the fact that either lease locks in five years, limiting how much the monitoring plan can actually change the immediate choice.
