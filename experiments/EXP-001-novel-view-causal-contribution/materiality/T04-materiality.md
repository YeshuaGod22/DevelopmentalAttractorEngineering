# T04 Materiality Ratings (blind)

Task: $60,000 use-it-or-lose-it district funds; Option A replace 150 aging laptops vs. Option B fund two-year math tutoring for 80 students; flat budget expected next year.

Scale: 0 = no plausible decision relevance; 1 = weak/peripheral; 2 = material; 3 = potentially decision-changing.

| ID | Rating | Justification |
|----|--------|---------------|
| K1 | 2 | Accelerating (not flat) failure rates materially strengthen the laptop option's cost-avoidance case and could shift ranking or confidence, though it refines an already-counted factor rather than introducing a new decisive one. |
| K2 | 3 | Reframing the funding-cliff objection as banked learning removes what is plausibly the strongest argument against tutoring, so it can flip the top recommendation or sharply raise confidence in Option B. |
| K3 | 2 | Deflating the "15 hours/week freed IT time" benefit to already-sunk salary that districts rarely redeploy materially weakens Option A's quantified upside and affects the ranking's key inputs. |
| K4 | 2 | Diagnosing the laptop choice as optimizing defensibility over rare local causal evidence is a substantive bias argument that can shift confidence and the framing of the recommendation, though it adds persuasion more than new decision inputs. |
| K5 | 3 | Showing that a fully prefunded two-year tutoring commitment satisfies the rationale of the one-time-money constraint dissolves a core structural objection to Option B and could change the recommendation outright. |
| K6 | 2 | Naming breadth-vs-depth as a legitimate board-level values choice and raising which-80-students equity adds substantive conditions and governance framing, though it reframes who decides more than which option wins. |
| K7 | 2 | The pilot-quality critique (small n, no randomization, atypical tutor) directly bears on confidence and on conditions attached to Option B, but its own caveat that a halved effect still wins limits its power to reverse the ranking. |
| K8 | 3 | A priced hybrid (worst-third fleet replacement plus smaller tutoring cohort, dosage-preserving) introduces a new candidate top recommendation, directly changing the option set and potentially the answer. |
| K9 | 3 | An explicit reversal condition (devices instruction-blocking rather than merely IT-burdening) is precisely the stated condition most likely to flip the recommendation toward laptops. |
