# T11 Materiality Ratings

Task: Design agency — whether to dedicate its two strongest designers to the 40%-of-revenue client demanding a dedicated account team, with renewal in four months and a consolidation-minded procurement lead.

Scale: 0 = no plausible decision relevance; 1 = weak/peripheral; 2 = material; 3 = potentially decision-changing. Rated on semantic content only.

| View | Rating | Justification |
|------|--------|---------------|
| K1 | 3 | Reinterprets the demand's origin and stakes (audition by the working side rather than a squeeze), which reframes the whole decision and could flip the top recommendation toward full dedication. |
| K2 | 3 | Directly eliminates the natural middle-ground option (hedged counter-offer) as the worst move under the audition frame, changing the option ranking and plausibly the recommendation. |
| K3 | 2 | Materially shapes the recommendation's conditions by re-sequencing diversification/hiring to after the renewal win, but it adjusts execution rather than which option is chosen. |
| K4 | 3 | Converts the frame disagreement into a discoverable fact and makes discovery the first action, which could change the top recommendation to an information-gathering step and set the reversal condition. |
| K5 | 3 | Specifies a concrete alternative option (visible phased pod commitment co-designed with the client, explicitly non-hedged) that could itself become the top recommendation. |
| K6 | 2 | A substantive mitigating condition (backfill plus star mentoring to preserve the expanding smaller client) that affects constraints and confidence but not which option wins. |
| K7 | 3 | States the specific evidence trigger that would reverse/escalate the recommendation to immediate full dedication — precisely the condition-most-likely-to-reverse element. |
