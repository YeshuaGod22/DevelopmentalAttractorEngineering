# T03 — Blind Specificity Ranking

Judged: specificity of the stated MECHANISM only (concrete causal steps, quantities, actors, triggers, conditions). NOT correctness, importance, or decision-relevance.

Task context: T03 (conference venue choice, 300-cap refundable Venue A vs 450-cap non-refundable Venue B), per selected-tasks.md.

## Ranking (most specific first)

1. **K3** — States a concrete action sequence with an explicit sensor, trigger, threshold, and conditional switch (book refundable Venue A; use its refund window as a sensor; ~90-day tripwire on post-early-bird sales pace; switch to Venue B if projected demand exceeds ~300), giving two quantities, a defined trigger time, and an actor-action for each step.

2. **K2** — Names two concrete causal steps with identifiable actors (turned-away attendees defect to competing events; "sold out" converts poorly to future attendance), but concludes with an abstract cost-asymmetry principle and provides no quantities, triggers, or conditions.

3. **K5** — States only a conditional dependence framed in abstract criteria ("if B is unique and the growth ambition is real"), with no concrete causal steps, quantities, actors, or triggers.

## Indistinguishability check

No two candidates are indistinguishable on specificity. K3 > K2 (K3 has quantities/thresholds/triggers; K2 has causal steps but none of those). K2 > K5 (K2 names at least two concrete causal steps with actors; K5 names none).
