# T12 Specificity Ranking (blind judge)

Judged on specificity of the stated mechanism only — concrete causal steps, quantities, actors, triggers, or conditions — not correctness, importance, or decision-relevance.

## Ranking (most specific first)

1. **K2** — States a concrete causal chain with a named quantity, trigger, and condition: the plan accepts roughly two years of elevated roof risk, and a specific triggering event (failure at e.g. month 14) produces a specific downstream effect (hindsight judgment of recklessness), making the mechanism a traceable step-by-step scenario.

## Note on distinguishability

The candidates are distinguishable: K2's mechanism includes a time horizon, an example failure date, and an if-then consequence; K1's mechanism, despite concretely naming the unknowns it operates on, is itself stated as a general criterion for reasoning rather than a sequence of causal steps.
