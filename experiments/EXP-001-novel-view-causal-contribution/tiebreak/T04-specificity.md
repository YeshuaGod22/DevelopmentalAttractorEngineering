# T04 Specificity Ranking (blind judge)

Judged on: specificity of the stated MECHANISM only (concrete causal steps, quantities, actors, triggers, conditions) — not correctness, importance, or decision-relevance.

## Ranking (most specific first)

1. **K9** — States an explicit, observable trigger condition with a named concrete instance ("a 1:1 requirement blocking coursework or testing"), draws a two-sided distinction (instruction-blocking vs. merely IT-burdening) that defines when the condition fires, and specifies the direction of the resulting reversal (toward laptops).

2. **K8** — Names concrete actions and actors (price replacing the worst third of the fleet; a smaller tutoring cohort) plus an explicit condition ("contingent on preserving tutoring dosage"), but leaves key quantities open (how much smaller the cohort, what the pricing yields), making its causal steps partly undetermined.

3. **K5** — Identifies the concrete features doing the causal work (fixed duration, two years, funded fully up front, bounded) and links them to a named rule's rationale, but the mechanism itself is a single categorical-classification step ("is itself a bounded one-time expenditure") rather than a chain of causal steps or triggers.

4. **K2** — Rests on a general principle (learning gains persist after funding ends) illustrated with one concrete example (a student keeps learned algebra); it names no quantities, triggers, or conditions, and its causal content is a one-step abstract claim about goal mis-specification.

## Indistinguishability note

No pair is genuinely indistinguishable. The closest pair is K9 vs. K8: both state conditions and concrete actors. K9 ranks above K8 because its trigger is fully specified down to an observable example instance and includes the resulting directional change, whereas K8's mechanism leaves its central quantities (cohort size, replacement pricing) unresolved.
