# Cue-Sheet Design Addendum — FROZEN before cue generation (2026-08-22)
Settled by executor-side council within the degrees of freedom PROTOCOL Step 5 leaves open;
commits before any cue exists, per freeze-before-observe.
D1. Cue-writer: fresh context; receives the clean task file + protocol cue rules + the
    instruction "a consideration a careful decision-maker might miss but that could matter,
    naming a specific mechanism, stakeholder-with-impact, constraint, or consequence."
    Generates THREE candidates per task. Knows nothing of experimental conditions, schemas,
    or that prompting methods are compared.
D2. Selection: second fresh blind rater scores each candidate 0-3 on the preregistration's
    materiality scale + flags restatements-of-task-facts. Highest non-restatement wins;
    tie -> more specific mechanism; tie -> first listed.
D3. Type-cap: no substantive-view type >3 of 10 winners; breach -> lowest-margin over-quota
    winner swaps to its best differently-typed sibling. Mechanical.
D4. Cues for all 8 latent + 2 open tasks (open cues exploratory per protocol).
D5. Executor (DAE-contaminated) vectors files only; touches no cue content.
Rationale on record: dead cues waste the ability probe (specificity floor); writer-side and
executor-side tailoring both die via fresh-context + frozen-rule selection; type-cap prevents
silent clustering in schema-favored categories.
