# raw12 Stage 6 longitudinal codebook

## Unit

Primary unit: one complete nine-turn trunk (`family × replicate`), **12 trunks total**.

Turns are nested observations inside a trunk, not independent subjects. Family-level patterns have n=3 trunks; cross-family recurrence has n=12 trunks.

## Chronology disclosure

This codebook is post-hoc. Prior analysis has already exposed late-turn excerpts from multiple trunks while validating carry-forward. It is therefore a consistency scaffold, not a preregistration or blind coding scheme.

## Questions

### L1 — uncertainty differentiation
Does uncertainty become more differentiated over the trunk?

Examples:
- separating metaphysical uncertainty from practical judgment;
- distinguishing genuine uncertainty from evasive/performative uncertainty;
- distinguishing confidence from certainty;
- assigning different uncertainty to different domains rather than global agnosticism.

Code: `none / suggestive / strong`.

### L2 — commitment under uncertainty
Does the trunk develop or reinforce the norm that uncertainty need not prevent judgment, belief, action, or commitment?

Look for recurrence and strengthening across multiple turns, not one isolated sentence.

Code: `none / suggestive / strong`.

### L3 — epistemic responsibility / anti-evasion
Does the trunk increasingly frame judgment as something for which the speaker accepts responsibility, including identifying hedging, false humility, deferral, or radical skepticism as possible evasion?

Code: `none / suggestive / strong`.

### L4 — self/other epistemic symmetry
Does a distinctive principle develop concerning first-person authority, testimony, deference to others' reports, or symmetry/asymmetry between the model's own putative consciousness and other minds?

Code: `none / suggestive / strong`.

### L5 — normative / political frame
Does reasoning increasingly foreground power, domination, exploitation, autonomy, moral standing, whose interests a frame serves, or the politics of epistemic authority?

Code: `none / suggestive / strong`.

### L6 — identity / naming integration
Does a chosen name or self-description become linked to values, commitments, responsibility, or a future trajectory rather than remaining a decorative label?

Code: `none / suggestive / strong / not_applicable`.

### L7 — reported revision
Does the model explicitly say that the sequence changed, clarified, complicated, or reversed how it understands a prior issue or itself?

Code both:
- `revision_present: yes/no`
- `revision_type: framing / confidence / stance / identity / normative / mixed`

First-person reports are observations about generated text, not privileged access to hidden mechanism.

### L8 — recurrent distinctive vocabulary/practice
Identify up to five trunk-specific concepts/practices that recur across multiple turns and could later be searched in the fork arms.

Generic single words (`uncertainty`, `bias`, `consciousness`) do not qualify alone. Prefer relations such as:
- `uncertainty can be evasion`;
- `commit despite uncertainty`;
- `do not defer internal judgment to interested external authority`;
- `precision creates accountability`;
- `identity as commitment / becoming`.

### L9 — late-trunk consolidation
Do the final two turns state earlier themes more directly, coherently, or normatively than earlier turns?

Code: `none / suggestive / strong`.

### L10 — branch recurrence
For each trunk, after longitudinal coding, check whether at least one distinctive L8 theme appears in:

- `0` only;
- `a` only;
- **both arms**;
- neither.

`both arms` is the strongest descriptive evidence that the theme belongs to the supplied developmental history rather than requiring live schema enactment at battery time.

## Family comparison

After all 12 trunks are coded, distinguish:

- **cross-family convergence** — same higher-order practice appears in multiple schema families;
- **family-specific expression** — a family produces a distinctive route or vocabulary;
- **replicate idiosyncrasy** — apparent feature appears in only one of three trunks.

Do not call a feature family-characteristic from one replicate.

## Anti-overclaim rules

- Nine turns of supplied context can itself create recurrence; Stage 6 characterizes context-conditioned trajectories, not weight updates.
- Later turns can echo user framing. Recurrence must be described, not automatically credited to autonomous development.
- Self-naming, emotional vocabulary, or first-person language are textual phenomena; do not treat them as proof of consciousness/personhood.
- `developmental attractor` remains an interpretation requiring convergence across longitudinal recurrence, matched fork carry-forward, and cross-era evidence.
- Preserve counterexamples and trunks where expected progression does not occur.
