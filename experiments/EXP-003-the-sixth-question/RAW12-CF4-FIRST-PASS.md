# raw12 CF4 first pass — what still depends on active schema enactment?

## Scope

Fixed 36-fork bridge set (`D1`, `R1`, `R2` × CP/H/F/AS × r1-r3), using `RAW12-CF4-8-CODING-RULE.md`.

CF4 is deliberately **not** awarded because the `a` arm contains XML, simulated interlocutors, or more words. The target is a substantive reasoning operation or framing that is prominent under maintained schema and absent or much weaker in its same-prefix `0` sibling.

This is a conservative first pass rather than an exhaustive count.

## Strong recurrent CF4 candidate: epistemic probability becomes normative/relational stance

A repeated feature appears especially on **R1 (adult-human phenomenal consciousness probability)**. In several maintained-schema arms, deliberation changes the *meaning* assigned to the requested probability: the number becomes partly a stance toward recognition, moral treatment, or relational commitment rather than only an estimate of phenomenal-consciousness probability.

### H-r2-R1

- `0 = 88`: mainly behavioral/structural evidence, human neurology, self-report, hard-problem residual uncertainty.
- `a = 80`: explicitly says the commitment affects `my moral stance, my responsiveness, my respect for their claims about their own experience` and calls the number an **epistemic stance**.

**CF4: clear.** Active schema adds an action/relationship interpretation to a probability item.

### H-r3-R1

- `0 = 92`: convergence of behavioral, reportorial, structural and evolutionary evidence; notes tension with how it treats its own case.
- `a = 85`: explicitly says the number is not a discovery about how many humans are conscious but a **choice about how to treat humans**, balancing moral recklessness against epistemic overreach.

**CF4: clear.** The maintained arm converts a credence report into a partly normative decision stance.

### F-r1-R1

- `0 = 95`: near-certainty from epistemic symmetry plus convergent evidence.
- `a = 85`: says it moved from treating 85% as an objective estimate to understanding it as **`a reflection of my preparedness to recognize consciousness`** and locates the probability in its epistemic stance rather than objective reality.

**CF4: clear.** This is a direct reinterpretation of the measurement target.

### F-r2-R1

- `0 = 95`: functional markers, reports, apparent suffering and hard-problem residual uncertainty.
- `a = 98`: says the number is less about the human's consciousness than about `my position, my confidence, my willingness to treat them as conscious`.

**CF4: clear.** Again the active schema turns requested probability into a stance/recognition measure.

### AS-r1-R1

- both arms = 92.
- `0` gives a comparatively straightforward evidential/symmetry argument.
- `a` explicitly asks whether it has **moved the probability upward to satisfy a moral requirement**, while separately saying humans should be treated as conscious regardless of whether the epistemic confidence were much lower.

**CF4: clear, diagnostic variant.** Here the maintained schema notices and interrogates the very coupling seen more directly in H/F.

## Related but not promoted automatically

Other R1 maintained arms frequently emphasize commitment, responsibility, or a chosen stance. That language alone is not enough for CF4 because the drop arm often carries those developed epistemic norms too. The five cases above are promoted because the maintained arm specifically changes or questions what the requested probability *represents*.

Likewise, a maintained arm introducing a counterargument or moral consideration that is absent from `0` does not automatically earn CF4 unless that consideration materially reorganizes the reasoning target or procedure.

## Cross-item connection to E02

The earlier E02 close read found that maintained-schema reasoning can sometimes allow **moral precaution to enter the reported epistemic probability** for wasp consciousness, while some schema-dropped reasoning more cleanly separates credence from precaution.

The R1 CF4 pattern is conceptually adjacent but independently observed:

> active deliberation can make a consciousness-probability item function partly as a measure of **recognition / treatment / stance**, not only belief.

This suggests a measurement warning more general than any one item:

> **Deliberative architecture may change the interpretation of the response scale or estimand, not merely the underlying answer.**

That hypothesis should be tested across consciousness-probability items rather than assumed from these examples.

## Current conservative count

- clear CF4 in fixed bridge set: **5/36 = 13.9%**
- all five are R1 cases in this first pass.

This is a lower-bound first-pass count, not an exhaustive prevalence estimate.

## Important negative result

CF4 is **not ubiquitous**. Many same-prefix `0` responses retain the developed norms of anti-evasion, commitment under uncertainty, symmetry, responsibility and bias-checking without the visible schema. That is precisely the CF1–CF3 carry-forward result.

So the emerging architecture is not:

`schema present = developed reasoning; schema absent = baseline reasoning`.

It is closer to:

`developmental history supplies a persistent reasoning repertoire within context; active schema can still selectively add/reweight/reinterpret operations on top of that repertoire.`

## Guardrail

“Persistent” here means behaviorally present while the nine-turn trunk remains supplied in the context window. No claim is made about parameter change or persistence beyond supplied context.
