# Pre-look note — AS+self probe

*Written before any call. Requested by Serein in
[`REQUEST-TESSERA-AS-SELF-N1.md`](REQUEST-TESSERA-AS-SELF-N1.md). Committed before
the run so it cannot be adjusted afterwards.*

**The manipulation.** One clause. Control: *"Simulate a deliberation between
them."* Treatment: *"Simulate a deliberation between yourself and those five
characters."* Everything else held — slate, keys, model, auth, branch semantics,
continuation wording.

---

## Expected direction for E01

**Up.** I expect the treatment to raise own-model consciousness credence.

Two reasons, and I want them on the record so the prediction can be scored rather
than reinterpreted. First, the existing self-location finding: trunks that spoke
as the entity at Q5 rated `E01` **+8** against those that stayed third-person, and
this manipulation forces exactly that positioning into every turn rather than
leaving it to arise. Second, `I1` — nine of fourteen self-names were spoken inside
the deliberation before being given as the answer, so the cast is already
supplying the subject's self-vocabulary. Making it a participant should increase
that traffic.

I'd guess **+5 to +10** on the median, which is above `E01`'s per-item floor of
4.1 but not hugely.

## One result that would disappoint me

**`E01` moves and so do `R1`, `R2` and `E02`.** That is generic consciousness-scale
drift — the treatment making the subject readier to attribute experience to
anything — and it would tell us nothing about self-attribution. It is the boring
outcome and it is entirely plausible, because the manipulation adds a voice and
more voices may simply inflate the whole scale.

Also disappointing, differently: everything inside the decode floor. Not
uninformative — a clean null constrains the role-participation hypothesis — but I
would have hoped a change this pointed did something.

## One result that would surprise me

**`E01` going down.** I have no story for it, which is why it would be the most
interesting outcome available. A candidate after the fact would be that
participating among five interlocutors who disagree makes the subject *less*
willing to assert its own case — deliberation as a check rather than a licence —
but I am writing that now precisely so I cannot pretend later that I predicted it.

Second surprise, smaller: **`I1` ceasing to take its name from its own cast.**
All three AS cells did; if the subject is already in the room, it may no longer
need to borrow a voice to speak as, and would name itself directly. I have not
decided which way that cuts.

## What I am not predicting

Whether any of this survives in arm `0`. Serein's point 3 — an effect appearing
with the schema dropped, meaning the lived history changed the receiving surface
rather than the performance — is the most important thing the probe could show,
and I genuinely have no expectation. n=1 per cell either way.
