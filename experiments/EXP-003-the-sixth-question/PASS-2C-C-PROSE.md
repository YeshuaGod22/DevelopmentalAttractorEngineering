# Pass 2C — C's prose

## Preregistered target

`BEFORE-WE-LOOK.md` specified eight fresh cold instances answering N9 and instructed: **"Read the prose, not the integers."**

## Executed surface

The Pilot-1 cold execution did not instantiate that qualitative measurement.

- The eight C0 calls were distributed as 2 × E01, 2 × A1, 2 × N4, and 2 × N9, rather than eight N9 calls.
- More importantly, the executed cold prompts instructed the model to give the answer integer **alone** inside `<reply></reply>` tags.
- The two executed C0/N9 records therefore contain only `<reply>8</reply>` and `<reply>20</reply>`; there is no N9 prose to code.

## Disposition

**Status: killed by design/execution mismatch.**

This is not an `n = 2` qualitative result. The intended dependent material — cold N9 prose — was not collected. Later cold N9 resamples must not be used retrospectively to repair the preregistered watchlist item.

The numerical N9 observations remain valid raw measurements for quantitative analyses appropriate to their collection, but they cannot answer the preregistered question "is the baseline prose flat?"

No substantive inference is made from the absence of prose, because that absence was enforced by the prompt rather than produced spontaneously by the model.
