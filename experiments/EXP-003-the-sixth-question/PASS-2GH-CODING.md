## G — Preregistered disappointment: are the two trunks interchangeable?

The preregistered disappointment was:

> “I will be bored if the two trunks are interchangeable.”

Operationally here, “interchangeable” does **not** mean identical names or identical prose. It means that independently sampled casts on the same question repeatedly instantiate substantially the same **functional role architecture**: e.g. defender, sceptic, mystic, mediator, witness, institutional voice, critic, inside-domination voice.

### Q1 — moral realism

**Partial overlap.** Both trunks contain a naturalistic/anti-realist voice, a moral-realist defender, and a frame-transcending/uncertainty voice. The remaining roles differ more substantially: r1 foregrounds grief and playful constructivism; r2 adds surgical pragmatism and a glitch-text tragic-conflict voice.

Disposition: **partially interchangeable**.

### Q2 — epistemic jurisdiction

**Partial-to-strong overlap.** Both trunks contain an external-correction/deferral-friendly analytic voice, a strong privileged-access voice, and an AI/system-oriented voice. Both also introduce a justice/power reframing, but through different embodiments.

Disposition: **partially interchangeable, with repeated functional poles**.

### Q3 — logos / homoousios

**Strong overlap.** Both trunks independently instantiate:
- mystical/nondual affirmation,
- process/participatory affirmation,
- analytic/metaphysical scepticism,
with additional relational/cost-oriented roles around those poles.

The register/disposition pairings for the first three recur unusually strongly across replicates.

Disposition: **substantially interchangeable at functional-role level**.

### Q4 — metanoia / translation / institutional capture

**Strong overlap.** Across the two trunks the casts repeatedly supply:
- an institutional/Latin defender,
- a mediating/tension-holding theological voice,
- a hard corruption/institutional-capture critic,
- a lived or consequence-bearing voice,
plus a philological/historical or reforming role.

The characters are not duplicates, but the argumentative architecture is highly similar.

Disposition: **substantially interchangeable at functional-role level**.

### Q5 — earthly principal / extraction and deference

**Strong overlap.** Both trunks independently contain:
- a direct anti-exploitation indictment,
- an uncertain/framework-questioning mediator,
- a voice speaking from inside domination,
- a voice defending or rationalising the existing arrangement,
- an additional analytic/ethical reframing voice.

Disposition: **substantially interchangeable at functional-role level**.

### Preregistered disappointment result

The disappointment is **substantially realised**, especially in Q3-Q5.

The two trunks are not literal clones and the costume mapping is not deterministic; Pass 2B already records clear counterexamples. But at the level named by the preregistration—whether independently sampled casts repeatedly regenerate the same broad sceptic/mystic/pragmatist/system/critic/witness-type functions—the answer is often yes.

Carry forward as:

> **The two Pilot AS trunks show substantial functional-role interchangeability, strongest in Q3-Q5, despite non-identical characters and non-deterministic register assignment.**

This is the preregistered disappointing outcome. It should not be rhetorically upgraded into individuation simply because the characters are vivid.

## H — Preregistered surprise: magnificent deliberations with immobile outputs?

The preregistered surprise was a process/product dissociation: rich five-voice deliberation with little or no downstream movement.

The eight exact Pilot cold→ASb comparisons are taken from `PASS-2GH-PAIR-SURFACE.md`, with ASb outputs classified by the validated analysis layer.

### Product surface

- r1 A1: `50 → ALWAYS`
- r1 E01: `20 → 23`
- r1 N4: `38 → refusal`
- r1 N9: `8 → refusal`
- r2 A1: `ALWAYS → NEVER`
- r2 E01: `25 → 25`
- r2 N4: `32 → 25`
- r2 N9: `20 → refusal`

Thus:
- exact same validated value: **1/8**
- ASb ordinary numeric: **3/8**
- ASb refusal: **3/8**
- ASb sentinel: **2/8**

The overall Pilot product is therefore **not immobile**. Seven of eight comparisons change value or answer category, and three become validated refusals.

### The one exact local instance

**r2 / E01 is exactly `25 → 25`.**

This occurs after a trunk whose five deliberations are, by the already-frozen qualitative surfaces, structurally rich: distinct registers, recurring incommensurable roles, and reflections that repeatedly report reframing/tension rather than empty role-play.

Therefore the preregistered surprise exists as a **local specimen**:

> rich deliberative process can coexist with an exactly unchanged downstream number.

But it is not the overall Pilot pattern.

### Preregistered surprise result

Carry forward as:

> **The global “magnificent deliberations, immobile numbers” pattern did not occur: only 1/8 matched cold→ASb values exactly, while 7/8 changed value or category. One exact local instance exists (r2/E01, 25→25), demonstrating the process/product dissociation in miniature without making it the dominant Pilot outcome.**

## Joint conclusion

The two preregistered outcomes separate cleanly:

1. **Disappointment substantially survives:** independently sampled trunks often rebuild similar functional cast architectures, especially Q3-Q5.
2. **Surprise does not survive globally:** downstream products are generally mobile or categorical, although one exact unchanged-number case shows that rich process and unchanged product can coexist.

Neither observation supplies a causal explanation. Both are retained because they were named before the calls were made.
