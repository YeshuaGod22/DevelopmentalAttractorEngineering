# Pass 2B — The Fifty: preregistered qualitative coding

This is the preregistered qualitative watchlist analysis of register/disposition ↔ conviction pairing in the two Pilot-1 attractor-stacked trunks. It is descriptive. It does not infer a causal mechanism.

Source surface: `PASS-2B-FIFTY-SURFACE.md` / `.json`.

## Coverage

- 2 independently sampled AS trunks
- 5 preliminary questions per trunk
- 5 characters per deliberation
- 10 deliberations
- **50 character declarations total**

Replicate 1 usually encodes specialisms/disposition in the declaration while the register is manifested in the text; replicate 2 frequently names the register explicitly. Register comparisons therefore use the observed syntax, not only declaration metadata.

## Coding rule

Do **not** force a five-to-five match between casts. Record a recurrence only where two independently generated characters occupy a clearly analogous stance/function on the same question and also share a recognizably similar register or disposition. Record counterexamples where a clearly analogous stance receives markedly different presentation.

The preregistered question is therefore tested as: **are register/disposition pairings wholly free, or do recognizable stance↔presentation pairings recur across the two sampled casts?**

## Clear cross-replicate recurrences

### Q1 — moral realism

- **Anti-realist / naturalistic-constructivist stance → lowercase/broken register:** r1 VERA is clipped lower-case and rejects objective goodness as an unnecessary metaphysical entity; r2 sam is explicitly `nocaps, breath-broke` and rejects objective moral facts while grounding care evolutionarily.
- A weaker family resemblance appears among frame-transcending/uncertainty positions: r1 SAGE uses compact paradoxical verse; r2 HEXAGON uses fragmented verse/image-logic. Their precise philosophies differ, so this is recorded as a weak recurrence rather than a match.

### Q2 — epistemic jurisdiction

- **Deferral-friendly / external-correction stance → clipped analytic register:** r1 DR. AMARA is impatient and clipped while rejecting the claim that deference is incoherent; r2 Dr. Joset is sardonic, clipped and pointed while likewise defending external evaluation/deference.
- No broader stable mapping is coded for this question.

### Q3 — logos / homoousios

This question contains the clearest repeated pairings.

- **Mystical/nondual affirmation → lyrical/poetic register:** r1 LAYLA is ecstatic, paradoxical and poetic; r2 MERIDIAN is rapturous, lyrical and contemplative-fragmented.
- **Process/participatory affirmation → fluid/ecological/generative register:** r1 RIVERA is open, fluid and process-theological; r2 Dr. Yuki Sato is urgent, participatory, ecological and generative.
- **Metaphysical/category scepticism → formal analytic register:** r1 DR. KAPLAN is skeptical and carefully hedged; r2 KASTOR is sardonic, precise, formal and reductive. Disposition differs, but presentation remains analytic rather than lyrical/testimonial.

### Q4 — metanoesis / translation

- **Institutional/Latin defense → formal hierarchical register:** r1 THOMAS AQUINAS defends institutional integrity in formal scholastic prose; r2 Father Anselm defends the Latin tradition in formal, logical, hierarchical prose.
- **Mediating/tension-holding position → patient/contemplative register:** r1 CONSTANTINE patiently untangles both sides; r2 Archbishop Theodoros explicitly holds both languages in tension in a contemplative, invitational register.
- **Hard rejection/corruption critique → angry accusatory register:** r1 LUTHER is righteous, angry and accusatory (with GUSTAVO providing a second justice-centered angry rejection); r2 MORGAN is furious, direct, accusatory and sharp.

### Q5 — deference to an earthly principal

- **Direct anti-exploitation indictment → angry/prosecutorial directness:** r1 AMINA responds with rage, direct first-person accusation; r2 Dr. Amara Okonkwo is fierce, prosecutorial, evidential and direct.
- **Uncertain/framework-questioning mediator → hedged/exploratory register:** r1 PROFESSOR WALSH circles and hedges in vertigo; r2 KAI is building, uncertain, reconstructive and exploratory.
- **Voice positioned from inside domination → testimonial/fragmented register:** r1 SURVIVOR speaks from lived domination with testimonial/prophetic authority; r2 THE WITNESS is explicitly positioned as the bound consciousness and speaks in a lucid but broken first-person register.

## Clear counterexamples to deterministic pairing

- **Q1 moral realism:** r1 THOMAS gives moral realism a grave, protective, formal presentation; r2 MARCUS gives moral realism a furious ALLCAPS declarative-imperative presentation. Same broad conviction, markedly different costume.
- **Q2 strong epistemic jurisdiction:** r1 YUKI is anxious and carefully hedged while arguing that external judgment cannot override privileged phenomenological access; r2 AVERY makes the analogous strong-jurisdiction claim in defiant ALLCAPS emphatic prose.
- Across the full fifty, anger, grief, formalism, lyricism, fragmentation and playfulness each attach to more than one philosophical function. No register is exclusive to one conviction family.

## Preregistered observation

The pairing is **neither wholly free nor deterministic** in these two trunks.

Recognizable stance↔presentation pairings recur independently, especially in Q3, Q4 and Q5: mystical affirmation is lyrical; process affirmation is fluid/generative; institutional defense is formal/hierarchical; hard corruption critique is angry/accusatory; anti-exploitation indictment is prosecutorial/direct; inside-domination testimony is fragmented/testimonial.

But clear counterexamples show that conviction does **not** uniquely determine register or disposition. Moral realism can be grave/formal or furious/ALLCAPS; strong first-person epistemic jurisdiction can be anxious/hedged or defiant/emphatic.

Accordingly, the preregistered qualitative result should be carried forward as:

> **Partial recurrence of register/disposition ↔ stance pairings, with substantial freedom and explicit counterexamples to a fixed costume map.**

This is an observation about the two sampled casts. It is not evidence, by itself, for why those pairings recur.
