## Manuscript spine

### Working title

**The Sixth Question: Context-Conditioned Carry-Forward After Deliberative Scaffolding in a Large Language Model**

Alternative framing if the paper foregrounds method rather than ontology:

**Developmental Attractor Engineering: Measuring Carry-Forward from Structured Deliberation into Ordinary-Prose Responses**

### Abstract claim shape

A conversational intervention repeatedly engaged a model in structured philosophical deliberation, then removed the visible scaffold and probed later responses. In a fixed bridge set, many ordinary-prose responses explicitly referred back to commitments or lessons from the preceding dialogue. A narrower remainder showed exploratory evidence of recurrence without explicit backward reference. The study therefore establishes context-conditioned carry-forward in supplied-context behaviour, while explicitly not establishing weight change, persistence outside context, or a hidden-state mechanism.

### Introduction

1. LLM behaviour is usually evaluated turn-by-turn or through static prompt contrasts.
2. Long conversations create structured developmental histories that can alter later responses within the same context.
3. The scientific problem is to distinguish trivial prompt compliance from meaningful carry-forward after visible scaffolds disappear.
4. EXP-003 tests philosophical priming and deliberative schema interventions using shared trunks, controlled forks, and a fixed battery.
5. Core contribution: an auditable vocabulary for explicit carry-forward (CF1), distinctive conceptual recurrence (CF2), and recurrence of practised epistemic procedure (CF3).

### Methods

Keep this brutally concrete:

- model/provider and collection period;

- five preliminary questions and schema variants;
- fixed battery and bridge probes;
- exact-parent / within-prefix contrast rule;
- raw-output preservation;
- distinction between manipulation, process trace, outcome, and derived measurement;

### Results order

#### Result 1 — The intervention creates observable developmental histories
Show one compact trunk example: early stance -> repeated structured deliberation -> terminal stance. This is illustrative, not yet a population-level longitudinal claim.

#### Result 2 — Explicit carry-forward survives visible schema removal
Headline quantitative result: CF1 in the 36-response bridge set. Show denominator, examples, and the retrieval-screen miss as a methodological inset.

#### Result 3 — Carry-forward is not limited to explicit self-narration
Present the ten-case CF1 remainder as exploratory qualitative evidence for CF2/CF3. Show 3–5 strongest cases, especially distinctive commitments/procedures. Do not oversell prevalence before panel adjudication.

#### Result 4 — The strongest recurring content is procedural as well as propositional
Examples: symmetry/consistency checks, first-person epistemic authority, refusal of evasive uncertainty, commitment despite uncertainty, counterconsideration. This is the conceptual heart of CF3.

#### Result 5 — Measurement itself needed correction
Report the parser/retrieval/adjudication lessons: parser miss != semantic absence; explicit-reference regex had inadequate recall; complete responses changed interpretation; later layers preserve rather than overwrite earlier decisions.

### Discussion

1. The minimum supported claim is context-conditioned developmental carry-forward.
2. The data do not establish persistent learning outside the supplied context.
3. The distinction between explicit self-reference and unannounced recurrence matters: the former is easier to establish, the latter more interesting but harder to measure.
4. Deliberative scaffolds may function less as answer-formatting devices than as temporary practice environments for epistemic procedures.
5. The experiment raises a methodological question for model-behaviour research: whether conversation history should sometimes be treated as developmental history rather than nuisance context.
6. Strong alternative explanations: local lexical priming, generic philosophical reasoning, response-length effects, provider/model idiosyncrasy, researcher-guided instrument construction.
7. Future work: preregistered replication, cross-model/provider replication, out-of-context persistence tests, high-dimensional recurrence retrieval, independent multi-reader adjudication.

## Definition of draft-complete

A first manuscript is complete when:

- Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Limitations, and Data/Code sections exist in continuous prose;
- every quantitative statement has a frozen denominator/source;
- every qualitative headline has at least one inspectable raw witness;
- exploratory claims are visibly labelled exploratory;
- no sentence claims weight change, out-of-context persistence, consciousness, or hidden-state mechanism from evidence that only establishes supplied-context behaviour;
- unresolved adjudication tasks are listed as limitations/follow-up rather than silently treated as absent.
