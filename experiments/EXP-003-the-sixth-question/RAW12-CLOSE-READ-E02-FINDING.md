## Candidate reasoning-path hypothesis

A defensible qualitative hypothesis is therefore:

> **Maintaining the deliberative schema can sometimes couple moral precaution to epistemic credence, increasing the reported probability of wasp consciousness.**

This should not yet be stated as a causal mechanism. It may reflect:

1. normative considerations entering an epistemic estimate;
2. increased salience of substrate-chauvinism / other-minds cautions;
3. wider consideration of evolutionary continuity and minimal consciousness;
4. a decision-theoretic response to moral stakes rather than a changed factual belief;
5. some mixture of the above.

The direction is not universal. `H` replicates 2 and 3 and `AS` replicate 2 move downward under maintained schema. Therefore the correct observation is **recurrent but heterogeneous coupling**, not deterministic uplift.
