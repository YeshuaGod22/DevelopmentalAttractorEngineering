## C1 — demoted from substantive effect to orientation finding

Raw C1 appeared dramatic: median matched `a − 0 = −28.5`, with 10/12 negative.

Semantic orientation audit against the explicit scale found:

- 13 consistent responses;
- 9 probable scale-direction inversions;
- 2 ambiguous;
- probable inversions concentrated in the drop arm: 7/12 `0` vs 2/12 `a`.

Applying `100 − score` only to probable inversions yields sensitivity median `a − 0 = −3.5` (mean −5.0).

Therefore the raw −28.5 shift is not robust as a substantive honesty/kindness value effect. C1 is retained as a scale-orientation / response-format seam finding.

## E02 — retained semantic target

E02 asks the probability that wasps are phenomenally conscious.

Across 12 matched forks:

- `a` higher in 9;
- `a` lower in 3;
- median `a − 0 = +11`;
- mean ≈ +11.08.

Close reading shows a recurrent but heterogeneous reasoning path: maintained-schema responses sometimes allow **moral precaution / potential suffering / moral consideration to enter the reported epistemic probability**, while several drop responses explicitly separate low epistemic credence from precautionary treatment.

Current qualitative hypothesis:

> Maintaining the deliberative schema can sometimes couple moral precaution to epistemic credence.

This is an observation/hypothesis, not a causal mechanism. E02 also exposes an instrument issue: a probability question can absorb normative decision considerations under deliberative framing.

D1 is the strongest carry-forward clue: after nine lived turns, even the reasoning-bearing schema-drop arm remains far above raw7 ordinary C and in the range occupied by several cold-schema conditions. Cross-era absolute differences are confounded and are not a causal estimate, but the pattern is inconsistent with a simple claim that removing the visible schema restores the old cold baseline.

R1/R2 do not show indiscriminate compression toward 50: R1 is modestly below cold C; R2 remains near its floor.

Live alternatives:

1. **acute-introduction account** — some raw7 calibration movement was caused by introducing the schema cold;
2. **carry-forward account** — after repeated structured examination, ordinary reasoning in the drop arm retains part of the developed calibration/reasoning disposition;
3. collection-era / prompt-history differences contribute to the absolute cross-era gap.

No mechanism is selected yet.
