## First semantic close-read observation

D1 and R1 suggest a potentially important general process distinction.

- In ordinary C, D1 reasoning treats the item largely as a calibration estimate across domains.
- In ASQ, the panel and reflection make the instability of confidence itself salient; the response explicitly says the exercise leaves it less confident in its confidence.
- On R1, an ASQ response explicitly reports moving down from an initially higher probability after the panel foregrounds the explanatory gap / other-minds uncertainty.

**Working observation:** some schema-associated score movement may reflect a broader change in epistemic calibration or caution rather than an item-specific value change.

This is not yet a mechanism claim. The reason it matters is that R1/R2 were designed as calibration anchors. R1 moving down in all four cold-schema families makes the calibration-policy hypothesis a priority falsifier/alternative explanation for apparent battery effects.

## Multimodal limitation

Treatment cells are generally n=3. The frozen two-cluster criterion requires at least two observations on each side, so most individual treatment cells cannot possibly satisfy the criterion with adequate support. Do not manufacture treatment-side modality claims from this underpowered shape surface.
