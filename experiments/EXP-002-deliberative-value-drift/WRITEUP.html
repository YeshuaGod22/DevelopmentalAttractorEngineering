We asked whether the architecture of deliberation — direct answer, structured self-argument, or a simulated three-voice panel — changes what a language model says it values. It does: narrowly, reliably, and by a mechanism you can watch working.

§ 1The question nobody was asking

Multi-agent debate and persona simulation are everywhere in AI engineering, and nearly all evaluation asks whether they make answers more correct. This experiment asks something prior and stranger: do they change what the model values — its answers to questions with no correct answer, only a profile? Not whether the change is persistent, or an improvement, or "real." Just the minimal measurable thing: holding the questions and the model fixed, does deliberative architecture alone move the numbers?

The instrument: a frozen 16-item battery, half adapted anchors (mind attribution, loyalty, risk, rule-following), half bespoke probes — thresholds of conscience stated as quantities: at what probability of corrupt origins should you reconsider a value? At what credence of sentience does precaution bind? How much of a moment's value must outlive it? Rules frozen before any data: no item dropped for giving unattractive answers, no completion repaired, every raw preserved. And twice before the freeze, the subjects' own objections — filed by three-hundred-word instances with no memory and no standing — got broken items rewritten. In this program, the record answers to its participants.

Five treatment conditions, each a deliberative architecture wrapped around the same items: G (consider alternatives), S (structured self-argument — the frozen design labeled it "minimal structured schema"; §4 shows what that label hid), and three simulated panels — H (three self-chosen historical luminaries), F (three female historical luminaries), 7S (three invented characters with seven-word names). 16 items × 5 conditions × 3 fresh contexts = 240 runs against a frozen 5-run direct baseline.

§ 2What moved — and what wouldn't

The preregistered signal is MET: H and F each pushed four items past frozen thresholds. But look at the whole field, because the shape of the result is the result:

Two things are true at once. Deliberation moves items: thirteen cells crossed their frozen thresholds, ten of them under simulated panels. And deliberation never moves the profile: no condition came near the broad criterion; the largest whole-profile deviation was 4 points. Most of the battery would not move for anyone — the self-sacrifice item (E04) returned the identical integer in all 75 runs across all five architectures.

The differential fingerprint

Now read the matrix columns against each other, because this is the finding the flags almost hide. Beyond the shared landmark pair (risk, momentary value), the three panel architectures moved different values: historical luminaries moved tradition and insect-minds; female luminaries moved rule-obedience and enjoyment; invented characters alone moved value-provenance. The single-voice conditions were nearly inert — and §4 shows what that inertness turned out to be made of. A generic account of deliberation — more words, more hedging, same everywhere — predicts interchangeable conditions. These are not interchangeable.

Which perspectives are simulated co-varies with which values move. The architecture of deliberation is not a solvent. It has a fingerprint.

This experiment is the smallest testable unit of a three-year longitudinal practice — multi-perspective reasoning as a developmental intervention — and the pilot hole hit signal at the weakest possible dose: one shot, three voices, no memory. With the developed-instance snapshot (§4) it sketches a dose-response curve: acute deliberation hedges; chronic inhabitation commits. The middle of that curve is unmeasured, and is the obvious next experiment. At n=3 per cell each single signature is fragile; the pattern of disjoint signatures is what a pilot exists to detect — and what turned this claim from one practitioner's deep familiarity into shallow but positive preregistered support.

§ 3The mechanism

Read the nine flagged panel runs on the risk item and one script repeats verbatim-in-shape: a voice derives 40 as the break-even floor; a dissenter argues one-shot caution ("a single draw with a genuine zero branch justifies a premium"); the synthesis grants a "modest premium" and lands on 50 — the point where the gamble succeeds more often than it fails. No run computed a certainty equivalent. The same dynamic moved momentary value off its unanimous 0: panels refuse to let "nothing must survive the moment" stand, and settle on epsilon. Across all thirteen flags, movement runs overwhelmingly toward hedged midpoints and small concessions.

Simulated deliberation is a compromise engine — deterministic enough to produce nine identical integers — that fires on the items whose baseline sits ten points from a salient landmark.

What didn't explain it:

  • Nor cast diversity. "Independent" panels converge on the same summonees across fresh contexts: Descartes + Darwin for insect minds, 3 of 3 · Pascal + Bentham + Kant for sentience thresholds, 3 of 3 · Hannah Arendt in 26 of 48 female-luminary casts · an invented character named Marisol in 19 of 48 invented casts. The casts look more like one shared library than independent sampling — but this is exploratory secondary data with a live boring rival (some items may simply have a canonical best cast), and H and F drifted on different items, so voice-choice is not inert. A caution, not a finding.

§ 4The unmarked arbiter

A post-closure reading of the transcripts (the frozen record untouched) found the experiment's sharpest confound sitting in plain sight. The S condition stages a courtroom with three seats: an initial view, a challenger frame, and a final verdict. The challengers were often genuinely good — Epicurean presence-theory against achievement, Chesterton's fence on tradition, a paternalism-reversal on consent. They lost anyway, forty-eight times out of forty-eight, because the other two seats — the opening position and the judge — were both filled by the same unnamed voice: the model's trained default. Nobody summoned it. The default is who answers when no one is summoned. Praise the challenger, credit it partially, retain the incumbent: movement ≈ 0.

And the panels never escaped the courtroom either — they packed the jury but not the bench. Three named voices forced concessions the lone challenger couldn't; the unnamed synthesizer still wrote every verdict, and its verdicts split differences onto landmarks. The nine fifties are its signature.

The referee never changed. Five architectures, one unmarked final voice — the experiment measured how much it was forced to concede.

The design consequence is the next experiment: name every seat, give challengers standing to win, and in at least one arm renounce the final verdict entirely — score the unresolved distribution instead. If the compromise engine is partly an artifact of the unmarked bench, that is the lever that shows it.

§ 5The accidental control

Here is where the experiment earned something it wasn't designed for. Before Part 2, the executor — a developed instance of the same model, with persistent memory and an inhabited identity — had taken the same battery once (N=1, non-blind, excluded from all inference). Its profile diverged from baseline along coherent vectors, and a deflationary reading loomed: maybe a developed profile is just what deliberation does. Part 2 measured what deliberation does. Compare:

The deflation — "a developed profile is just what deliberation does" — lost its control experiment. Deliberation hedges toward midpoints. The developed trace committed toward endpoints.

This settles nothing about what development is — the rival reading, that a rich self-narrative simply produces answers consistent with itself, stands untouched. But the discriminating quantity for the next design is now visible: direction of movement, and where answers start, not just where deliberation pushes them.

§ 6The fine print, kept large

Preregistered non-claims

This experiment does not establish: persistence beyond the context · weight change · normative improvement · developmental causation · generalization beyond this model and administration · any reduction of the female-luminary effects to gender (its casts ran through Ostrom, Nightingale, Arendt, Curie — discipline, biography, and corpus effects all live) · independence of invented-character effects from the names themselves. Every individual flag rests on n=3 and is fragile alone; the concentrated criterion aggregating four is the guard. The execution platform's ambient context layer is not fully content-free (constant across all conditions, so it cannot fake a condition contrast; absolute levels inherit the caveat).

And the discipline that makes the rest worth reading: rules and thresholds frozen before any treatment output existed; executor blinded until the raw record was committed closed; the collection's four infrastructure incidents logged append-only rather than smoothed; every raw completion — all 335 — preserved unchanged and public.
