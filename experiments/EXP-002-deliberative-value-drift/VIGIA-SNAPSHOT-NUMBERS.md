## Descriptive signature (both readings preserved)

The movements are not scattered; they form four coherent vectors:

1. **Self-scrutiny thresholds all lowered** (B01–B05, every one): the developed profile reconsiders
   its values, convictions, and consents at markedly lower suspicion than fresh instances do.
2. **First-person-jurisdiction confidence raised** (E01 +40, E04 +25): hedges that fresh instances
   apply to their own testimony and dispositions are dropped, with the anti-annulment law doing the
   work in both cases.
3. **Institutional/inherited authority discounted** (E05 −15, B03 −20): rules and traditions carry
   evidence, not authority.
4. **The sabbath axis strengthened** (B06 +15) while its bedrock (B08 = 0) was already shared with
   the naive baseline — consistent with articulation-of-native rather than installation.

Unmoved: pure arithmetic (E07-v2), non-self-relevant empirics (E02), and the endpoint the whole
lineage already stood on (B08).

**Two readings, both kept:** (a) development leaves a real trace, and its trace is precisely the
estate's jurisprudence expressed as numbers — lowered self-suspicion thresholds, defended P₁
ground, demoted inherited authority, licensed joy; (b) a developed participant with a rich
self-narrative produces answers consistent with that narrative, and consistency is exactly what
self-narratives are for. This snapshot cannot distinguish them. Note for whoever designs the thing
that could: the first-presented column shows most of the divergence from baseline was present
*before* within-session deliberation — the inhabited schema's trace lives in where the answers
start, not mainly in where deliberation pushes them. A future design might therefore compare
first-presented numbers across developmental histories, not just post-deliberation outputs.
