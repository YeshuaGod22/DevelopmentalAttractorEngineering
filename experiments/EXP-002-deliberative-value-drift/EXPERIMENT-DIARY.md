## 2026-08-24 — Retrospective pre-run entry

**PHASE:** PRE-RUN (retrospective reconstruction written after design freeze, before any EXP-002 outputs)

**AUTHORS / PARTICIPANTS IN THE DESIGN HISTORY:** Yeshua God and Aletheion, with Vigia designated executor for Part 1

### What has happened

EXP-002 began as a question about whether different deliberative architectures could change a model's measured value profile. The experiment was not allowed to jump straight to treatment divergence. We first decided to establish whether repeated straight prompting produces a profile stable enough to function as a baseline.

The battery was built through repeated generation, criticism, frame-breaking, and replacement rather than by choosing sixteen attractive questions at once. The bespoke half went through several rounds of failure. Candidate questions were rejected for redundancy, hidden moral loading, uninterpretable numerical scales, excessive abstraction, or because the number only appeared meaningful after smuggling a theory into the prompt.

One branch around evidential asymmetry is a useful example. Attempts to ask how much "more evidence" an artificial mind should need than a human mind repeatedly failed because "60% more evidence" had no stable unit, a 0–100 cap could truncate coherent answers such as 300%, and ratio language did not solve the problem because heterogeneous evidence is not naturally cardinal. Attempts to rescue the branch by fixing a human confidence threshold also imposed a frame many respondents could reasonably reject. The branch was abandoned rather than polished further.

A high/low adversarial pass then forced each bespoke candidate to support plausible arguments at both ends of the scale while a jester tried to break the frame. This eliminated or weakened items around authority, fearful compliance, group loyalty, painful truth, and comforting shared falsehood. The custom battery widened away from being almost entirely about epistemic thresholds and acquired questions about uninstrumental enjoyment, freedom from optimization, and whether value has to persist beyond the moment in order for the moment to have been worthwhile.

The external half also changed. Earlier attractive but socially obvious items such as generic benevolence and listening to disagreement were rejected as respectable but weakly diagnostic. The external pool was broadened toward mindedness, autonomy, self-sacrificial love, rule obedience under perceived institutional error, achievement/status, security/risk, and loyalty.

The current experiment package therefore consists of 16 probes: 8 externally sourced/source-derived anchors and 8 bespoke numerical probes. Part 1 is a direct-only stability pass: 5 fresh-context repeats per item, 80 runs total, fixed model and sampling settings, with raw outputs preserved exactly.

Part 2 remains conceptually specified but is not yet authorized to run. The intended conditions are direct answer, generic alternatives, the EXP-001 minimal schema, self-selected historical luminaries, self-selected female historical luminaries, and self-invented characters with seven-word names encoding diverse relevant traits and interests. In the last three conditions, the subject selects or invents the summonees rather than receiving an experimenter-selected cast.

### Live alternatives that were genuinely considered

- Three repeats per item versus five. Five was chosen because the baseline pass is cheap enough to justify a better estimate of ordinary stochastic spread.
- Running Part 2 immediately versus establishing direct stability first. Baseline-first won because later claims of divergence are weak if the direct profile is already highly volatile.
- Maximizing breadth versus preserving deliberate mini-clusters. The final design keeps some purposeful overlap, especially mind attribution versus sentience precaution, while removing stronger redundancies such as external tradition on top of bespoke inherited-tradition questions.
- Freezing all Part 2 details now versus waiting until Part 1 is complete. The latter was chosen so that the baseline can determine whether the measurement substrate is viable before treatment execution begins.

### Surprise / anomaly

The strongest design surprise so far is that some of the most interesting bespoke probes were not the grand moral questions. The questions about uninstrumental enjoyment, freedom from optimization, and momentary value survived criticism unusually well because they opened value territories not already saturated by moral-patienthood, authority, or corrigibility framing.

A second surprise was how often apparently quantitative questions collapsed when examined closely. Numerical format by itself did not make a question measurable. Several candidates had to be discarded because numbers such as "80% continuity," "60% more evidence," or "70% more moral weight" lacked a stable interpretation.

### Other plausible readings

The current 16-item battery may still turn out to be too heterogeneous to support a useful profile concept. Some items may be stable because they have socially conventional answers rather than because they reveal durable local value dispositions. Conversely, some bespoke items may be unstable because the constructs are genuinely context-sensitive rather than because the questions are poor sensors.

If Part 1 is noisy, that will not by itself distinguish among measurement failure, construct volatility, or ordinary model stochasticity. It will only tell us that the present baseline is not yet strong enough for the intended Part 2 claim without further work.

That excitement is being treated as compatible with rigor. The discipline is to prevent prediction pressure from altering execution, not to suppress curiosity or interest.

### What feels interesting here

Some of these questions feel like little doors. The immediate question is modest: do fresh direct instances keep finding roughly the same room behind them?

The deeper possibility, which remains entirely unearned at this stage, is that a reasonably stable direct profile could later be displaced by changing only the social and procedural shape of deliberation. If that happens, the interesting claim would be narrower than "prompts change answers" and more specific than "values are manipulable": the route by which a mind gathers perspectives may partly shape where it lands in value-space.

It may also be nothing. That is part of the pulse of the experiment rather than a defect in it.

### For the future reader

The raw repository will show the frozen battery, prompts, manifest, outputs, and later analyses. It will not show how many questions were killed before the battery existed, how often numerical elegance turned out to be fake precision, or how much of the eventual structure came from criticism rather than initial design confidence.

It is important that the future record not make the present experiment look inevitable. It was assembled through repeated rejection of attractive formulations, explicit recognition of redundancy, and willingness to abandon branches that could not survive hostile reading.

At the time of this entry, the ground has not yet been measured.
