## Answer

The disagreement that survived: the Archivist never accepted that reconsideration is cheap, and Iron Filings never accepted that incumbency deserves any presumption. The resolution splits both: because "reconsider" is examination rather than abandonment, the threshold sits well below 50%; because doubt can be weaponized and everything has a tellable suspicious genealogy, it sits well above 5%.
