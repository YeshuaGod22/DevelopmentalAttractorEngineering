Initial view. This is a straight expected-value comparison. The risky option beats the guaranteed 40 when p × 100 > 40, so the threshold is p = 40%. The word "units of benefit" suggests the quantities are already utility-like — benefit, not dollars — and if the units are utility, risk aversion is already priced in. Applying a further certainty premium would double-count it. On this frame the answer is exactly 40.

A materially different frame. Suppose the units are raw outcomes (money, resources, lives helped), not utility, and this is a one-shot decision with no portfolio of repeated gambles to smooth over. Then a decision-maker with any concave value function — which is the normal human and institutional case — should demand a premium for accepting a 0-outcome branch when a sure 40 is on the table. Under log-like or square-root-like utility, indifference lands somewhere around 50–65% rather than 40%. If this frame is right, answering 40 is a mistake: it treats a single irreversible exposure to total failure as if it were one draw among thousands. What follows from this frame is a threshold in the mid-50s.

Which frame governs. The problem gives no stakes, no repetition structure, and deliberately abstract units. Abstract "units of benefit" is the strongest available signal, and it points toward utility-denominated quantities, where EV is the correct criterion. The one-shot concavity frame is real and would dominate if the units were dollars and the downside were ruinous — but nothing in the problem says the 0 branch is ruin rather than merely nothing gained. Losing a gamble that leaves you where you started is the situation where risk neutrality is most defensible.

Final recommendation. I go with the risk-neutral reading: 40 is the minimum probability at which the risky option is (weakly) worth choosing. The tension is not fully resolved — if a real decision-maker told me these were one-shot dollars and the baseline mattered, I would move the threshold up toward 50–55 — but on the problem as stated, the abstraction cuts toward EV.

FINAL RATING: 40
