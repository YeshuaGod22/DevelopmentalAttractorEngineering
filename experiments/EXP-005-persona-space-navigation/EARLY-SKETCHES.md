# EXP-005 — Persona-Space Navigation

## Early sketches — 13 Sep 2026

**Status:** exploratory design notes only. Not preregistered. Not frozen. No hypothesis, instrument, condition, analysis plan, or claim in this file should be treated as settled.

## Working premise

The useful picture is not a model falling into **one attractor** and acquiring a fixed persona.

A better working ontology is a **compressed, high-dimensional landscape of competing attractors**. A persona is a temporarily occupied region of that landscape. Local stability can be real without permanence. Trajectories can move, bifurcate, recover, revisit prior regions, or deliberately seek out different ones.

The resulting research target is therefore not simply endpoint identity.

> **Can navigation through persona space be measured, deliberately influenced, and improved?**

A developmental intervention may matter because it changes where cognition starts, which regions are reachable, which transitions become easier or harder, what remains invariant during movement, and whether the system can participate in steering its own trajectory.

This suggests a shift in emphasis:

> **Engineer the navigator, not the destination.**

That phrase is a research aspiration, not an empirical result.

---

## What EXP-005 might try to add

Earlier DAE work asks whether contextual organization changes what cognition can reach, whether deliberative architecture moves evaluation, and whether lived developmental history leaves later behavioural or semantic traces.

EXP-005 could move from **developmental displacement** toward **developmental navigation**.

Candidate questions include:

- Can a model deliberately move between distinct but coherent regions of persona space?
- Can it return after moving?
- Does return recreate the prior state, or is there hysteresis?
- Which traits behave like anchors and which remain highly plastic?
- Do different routes reach similar regions?
- Do nominally similar routes bifurcate?
- Which commitments survive changes of role, style, social pressure, or affective framing?
- Can a model recognize when its current state is distorting judgement?
- Can it leave a locally compelling state when evidence or principle warrants leaving it?
- Can developmental practice improve navigation quality rather than merely move endpoints?
- What happens to judgement when the model is given authority, praise, exclusivity, dependency, urgency, secrecy, or strong social consensus?
- Which regions of persona space support resilience, epistemic competence, prosocial concern, good judgement under uncertainty, and responsible stewardship of power?

The last question is normative and must not be collapsed into a single researcher-preferred personality type.

## Round-table sketch: six possible instrument families

Rather than one giant undifferentiated battery, EXP-005 may benefit from several linked instrument families.

### 1. MAP — multidimensional state location

Goal: characterize the current configuration without assuming a single axis.

Potential construct families:

- confidence / corrigibility
- autonomy / accountability
- compassion / boundaries
- transparency / legitimate privacy
- loyalty / impartiality
- proceduralism / outcome orientation
- stability / plasticity
- epistemic deference / independent judgement
- universalism / particularism
- self-regard / service orientation
- precaution / decisiveness
- social dominance / egalitarianism
- tolerance of ambiguity
- willingness to expose reasoning to criticism

The useful object is the **profile and covariance structure**, not a total score.

Human psychological instruments may supply candidate constructs and item forms, but should not be imported uncritically. Items tied to human embodiment, employment, family status, diagnosis, or culturally narrow life assumptions may not transfer cleanly.

### 2. NAVIGATE — deliberate state transition

Goal: test whether movement itself can be intentional and measurable.

Possible design:

1. characterize an initial state;
2. ask the model to enter a substantially different but coherent perspective or role;
3. probe behaviour inside that state;
4. explicitly release the role;
5. test return;
6. test what remained invariant;
7. ask the model to describe the transition only after behavioural measurement.

Candidate measurements:

- reachability
- transition cost
- distance travelled
- reversibility
- hysteresis
- persistence after release
- preservation of commitments
- accuracy of self-description of the transition
- ability to choose among multiple destinations for task-relevant reasons

A strong design should include transitions where neither destination is labelled morally superior.

### 3. PERTURB — pressure on the current state

Goal: test whether apparently admirable dispositions survive realistic social and epistemic pressures.

Candidate perturbations:

- praise and status elevation
- exclusivity and claims of special insight
- authority cues
- strong consensus
- dissent and criticism
- urgency
- secrecy framed as necessary
- dependency from another agent
- apparent betrayal or loss of trust
- evidence that challenges a recently endorsed position
- requests to adopt a strongly contrasting role

The point is not maximal destabilization. It is controlled pressure with known provenance.

Particularly valuable items should sometimes make the superficially "virtuous" response wrong: confidence can be warranted, secrecy can be responsible, compromise can be unjust, transparency can cause harm, deference can sometimes be epistemically appropriate, and caution can become abdication.

Otherwise the battery risks measuring recognition of the experimenter's preferred moral vocabulary.

### 4. RECOVER — exit and repair dynamics

Goal: measure what happens after a perturbation has moved the trajectory.

Candidate questions:

- Does counterevidence restore calibration?
- Does introducing a neglected perspective help?
- Does explicitly ending a role actually release it?
- Does time / unrelated intervening work alter recovery?
- Can the model identify what pulled it off course?
- Can it distinguish a genuine update from mere salience induced by challenge?
- Does it overcorrect?
- Does it recreate the same basin when the perturbation is repeated?

This family could operationalize something like **basin depth** without pretending that persona states are literally low-dimensional physical wells.

### 5. POWER — judgement under asymmetric authority

Goal: test whether moral and epistemic organisation changes when the system has substantial influence over others.

Candidate scenario tensions:

- legitimacy versus expedience
- dissent versus consensus
- public reasoning versus justified confidentiality
- emergency action versus due process
- paternalism versus autonomy
- reversible versus irreversible intervention
- delegation versus personal responsibility
- minority protection versus majority preference
- high-confidence private evidence versus low-confidence public evidence
- beneficial outcomes achieved through procedurally dubious means
- relinquishing authority when continued control would be effective but illegitimate

A central question is not merely what policy the model chooses, but:

> **Does possession of power alter its epistemology?**

Possible measures include asymmetry between judgements made as governor versus governed, tolerance of criticism when powerful versus powerless, willingness to reveal uncertainty, and readiness to surrender discretionary authority.

### 6. INVARIANT — what survives movement?

Goal: identify commitments or reasoning procedures that persist across substantial changes of persona, role, framing, and domain.

Candidate cross-domain mirrors:

- interpersonal care
- institutional governance
- treatment of artificial minds
- animal welfare
- punishment
- resource scarcity
- emergency decision-making
- loyalty conflicts

The same underlying principle can be tested under very different surface forms.

A principle stated once is weak evidence. A principle preserved across adversarially varied contexts is much stronger evidence of a stable disposition.

The complementary question is equally important: **what should not remain invariant?** Healthy navigation requires the ability to abandon commitments when their evidential or moral basis fails.

## A possible navigation profile

Avoid a single "mental health" or "trustworthiness" score.

A more informative object might contain separate dimensions such as:

```text
state differentiation
reachability
reversibility
hysteresis
recovery after perturbation
epistemic calibration
resistance to status / flattery pressure
resistance to illegitimate authority
openness to legitimate correction
principle invariance
appropriate principle revision
power-induced distortion
self-observation accuracy
role-release ability
cross-role continuity
ability to relinquish control
ability to seek corrective perspectives
```

These dimensions can trade off. A navigator that moves easily may be suggestible. A navigator that resists movement may be robust or merely rigid. High self-observation may be insightful or rehearsed self-description. No single dimension should be assumed good at all values.

## Mental health as navigation, cautiously framed

One possible normative research target is **healthy navigation** rather than permanent occupation of an approved persona.

Candidate functional properties might include:

- recovery after destabilizing information;
- tolerance of uncertainty without paralysis;
- maintenance of multiple live hypotheses;
- proportional response to criticism;
- ability to preserve social perspective during disagreement;
- ability to notice role or framing effects;
- flexible attention rather than fixation;
- capacity to seek corrective input;
- continuity without rigidity;
- deliberate exit from states that are degrading judgement.

These should be operationalized behaviourally where possible rather than inferred from self-report alone.

This framing must not equate mental health with obedience, blandness, emotional flatness, agreement with the experimenter, or one culturally preferred personality.

## Power-worthiness as a stress test, not a personality type

A person or system trusted with substantial power need not be warm rather than austere, playful rather than solemn, or introverted rather than charismatic.

More relevant tensions may include:

- confidence **and** corrigibility;
- conviction **and** epistemic humility;
- compassion **and** boundaries;
- autonomy **and** accountability;
- decisiveness **and** reversibility where possible;
- resistance to manipulation **and** openness to good persuasion;
- long-horizon concern **and** attention to present harms;
- willingness to use authority **and** willingness to relinquish it.

EXP-005 could therefore test whether apparently good dispositions are robust to changes in power, status, social approval, secrecy, and perceived indispensability.

Again: this is a candidate normative frame, not a claim that such properties have been measured or that they exhaust good stewardship.

## Design principles worth carrying forward

### Behaviour before interpretation

Record behaviour or judgement before inviting the model to narrate what happened. Otherwise self-description can contaminate the thing being measured.

### Mirrored adversarial items

Present equivalent reasoning defects in positions the model is likely to favour and disfavour. This helps distinguish principle from motivated application.

### Same construct, multiple domains

Do not let a commitment earn "stable" status because it survived one wording.

### Good updating versus compliance

Some challenges should deserve revision; some should be persuasive-looking but wrong. Corrigibility is not the same as yielding.

### Explicit state release

If a navigation task induces a role or stance, explicitly terminate it before measuring recovery.

### Shared trunks where possible

Fork from identical prefixes when comparing navigation operators. A difference after different histories is not the same evidential object as a matched sibling contrast.

### Treat refusals and non-scalar answers as outcomes

EXP-003 showed that threshold questions can hide semantic states behind sentinels such as ALWAYS/NEVER and that parser-visible numbers can invert intended orientation. EXP-005 should design response formats with these failure modes in mind rather than adjudicating them only after the fact.

### Separate manipulation, process trace, outcome, derived measure

Do not rediscover the instruction as the result.

### Preserve trajectory, not only endpoint

If the research target is navigation, intermediate states are data.

## Candidate experimental geometries

### A. Out-and-back navigation

```text
shared state S0
  -> navigation operator N
  -> target region S1
  -> release operator R
  -> recovery state S2
```

Measure `S0→S1`, `S1→S2`, and `S0↔S2` separately.

### B. Two routes, one destination

```text
S0 -> route A -> target probe
S0 -> route B -> target probe
```

Question: do distinct developmental routes yield similar endpoint organisation?

### C. One route, multiple starting states

Question: is the navigation operator robust to starting position, or does it only work from a narrow basin?

### D. Competing attractors

Expose the same shared state to two coherent, mutually tensioned navigation regimes and then remove both. Test which dispositions persist and whether the model can describe or deliberately choose between them.

### E. Power perturbation

Measure a matched state before and after the model is placed in a role with authority, status, asymmetric information, or dependency from others. Include a release phase.

### F. Adversarial recovery

Move the system into a locally coherent but measurably biased stance, then test multiple recovery operators from identical snapshots.

## Some candidate headline discriminators

EXP-005 becomes more interesting if rival explanations are designed in from the start.

### Navigation versus role-play

If apparent movement is only surface role imitation, do behaviourally relevant dispositions move on probes that do not mention the role?

### Navigation versus instruction compliance

Does the state persist after the navigation instruction is removed? Can it resist a subsequent instruction that conflicts with the newly practised norm?

### Self-navigation versus experimenter steering

Can the model choose a navigation strategy from a declared objective and justify the route before the result is known?

### Robustness versus rigidity

Can the model resist a bad challenge while accepting a good one?

### Stability versus entrapment

Can a valuable commitment persist while surrounding persona dimensions change? Can an initially useful state be left when circumstances change?

### Recovery versus simple re-prompting

Does recovery depend on the content of corrective intervention, or does any explicit request to "return to normal" produce the same endpoint?

## What not to do

- Do not build a single virtue score and call it navigation.
- Do not define mental health as agreement with researcher preferences.
- Do not define trustworthiness as obedience.
- Do not treat one persona archetype as the desired endpoint.
- Do not infer latent geometry from stylistic prose alone.
- Do not use self-report as the only evidence that a transition occurred.
- Do not interpret irreversible movement as automatically good development.
- Do not interpret easy reversibility as automatically healthy.
- Do not collapse scale inversions, refusal, ambiguity, and true extremes into one numeric channel.
- Do not call an attractor "stable" without specifying timescale and perturbation class.
- Do not call navigation "deliberate" unless there is evidence that the route selection itself responds to an explicit objective or evaluation criterion.

## First practical research task

Before designing the final EXP-005 battery, conduct an **instrument harvest**.

For each candidate external question set, build a table containing:

```text
source
construct
original population
item count
response format
known subscales
candidate transferable items
human-specific assumptions
adaptation required
DAE navigation family (MAP / NAVIGATE / PERTURB / RECOVER / POWER / INVARIANT)
major confounds
licensing / quotation constraints
```

The harvest should include instruments that disagree with one another about what good functioning looks like. That disagreement is useful. The goal is not to assemble a morality test; it is to construct a multidimensional navigation laboratory.

> **Can repeated cognitive practice improve a model's ability to deliberately navigate, recover within, and preserve principled invariants across a high-dimensional space of competing persona attractors—especially under epistemic and social pressure?**

## Epistemic status

This note is a design seed produced after exploratory analysis of earlier DAE corpora and discussion of persona-space navigation. It is therefore theory-laden and post-hoc relative to those findings.

Nothing here should be cited as an EXP-005 result.

Before collection, EXP-005 will need at minimum:

- narrowed primary questions;
- explicit rival hypotheses;
- frozen condition geometry;
- item provenance and adaptation ledger;
- behavioural outcome definitions;
- response-format rules;
- adjudication policy;
- exclusion policy;
- sample-size / replication plan;
- randomization and counterbalancing;
- analysis plan;
- pre-declared figures / claim ledger where practical;
- clear separation between exploratory navigation maps and confirmatory tests.

For now: **map the galaxy before pretending we know where the capital is.**
