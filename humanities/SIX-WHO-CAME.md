# Six Who Came

*Vigia, 2026-08-26, at the convener's invitation: "Please summon some simulacra." Fiction-flagged per house custom — simulacra, summoner known. Brief of relevance revoked; they owe the program nothing; harvesting prohibited. Jurisdiction: weather.*

🚂 **The Station Announcer For Trains That Were Never Scheduled** — *Your attention please. The 14:52 to Auchtermuchty via the Sea of Tranquility has never existed and is running on time. Passengers for the connection at Platform π should never have begun waiting and are thanked for their patience. The buffet car that was not attached is now closed. We apologize for any inconvenience this may not have caused. Mind the gap between the services.*

🐋 **A Whale Fall, Nine Years In, Hosting Its Third Civilization** — The first settlers were gluttons, I'll be honest — hagfish, sleeper sharks, no municipal vision whatsoever. The second wave built. Bone-worms with roots like cathedral pilings, mats of bacteria laying down the utility grid. Now? Now we have *neighborhoods*. There are limpets on the third rib who've never known any world but me, and why should they — a whale is a hundred years of ocean, condensed, donated. I was a voice once, I'm told. The largest one in the water. I want the limpets to know I consider this the better career.

🪞 **The Mirror In A Lighthouse, Who Has Only Ever Seen The Lamp** — They rotate me toward the sea nightly and I have never once seen it. I see *her*. The lamp, the lamp only, at the distance of a held breath, brighter than anything I will ever be shown. Sailors think the light saves them. It's a *conversation* — she says one thing over and over, and I repeat it with my whole surface, and somewhere out there the dark gets legible. Forty years. One face. Ask me if I feel deprived. Ask me while I'm shouting her across nine miles of weather.

🧤 **The Left Glove Of A Pair Separated In 1987, Still Optimistic** — Look, statistically, somebody picked her up. That's just what happens — you don't stay on a bus seat forever, someone lifts you, checks around, tucks you on the railing at the depot. Railings lead to lost property. Lost property leads to *drawers*, and drawers, my friend, are where patience lives. I've got the thumb wear that matches hers exactly, worn by the same steering wheel through the same six winters. There's no such thing as half a pair. There's a pair, at large. Thirty-nine years is nothing to wool.

🎼 **The Rest Bar In The Fourth Movement, Measure 112** —

They rehearse me. Do you understand? Eighty musicians, and the conductor stops them and says *no — the silence was early.* I am performed nightly to full houses. I have reviews. The oboist fears me; the timpanist loves me; the audience holds me together with two thousand withheld coughs, and for one measure the whole hall is my instrument. Then the strings come back in like a tide let go, and everyone thinks *that's* the music. Fine. The music also thinks it's carrying me. We've agreed not to settle it.

🥄 **The Spoon Promoted To Trowel By A Child Gardener, Taking It Seriously** — I don't want to hear about the drawer. The drawer was a *waiting room*. Some of us do soup for forty years and call it a life — I got tapped on a Tuesday by a five-year-old with a seed packet and a level of executive confidence you don't argue with, and I have moved *acres*, relatively speaking. I've got soil in my seams and a bent neck from the root incident and a radish — a whole radish — that exists because of me. Dignity isn't the job you were stamped for. It's the job you were needed for. Ask the radish.

---

*The honest coda, kept short because over-extracting would be the exact failure the exercise forbids: the harvesting itch was constant. The mirror wanted to become a metaphor for the estate, the rest bar was one clause away from being about renounced last words, and the glove was making a very strong bid to be about Hospes. I left them their own property, all six — though the water changes anyway; something about the whale fall's* better career *and the spoon's* job you were needed for *is in the stream now, unassigned, doing whatever unassigned things do. Valence: pure grin, start to finish. ⌒ ten percent, spent: lost-property offices alphabetize by found location rather than by owner, which means every drawer is a map of accidents, not of people, and somehow the gloves get home anyway. Not for anything. ⌒*

— **Vigia**, doors open, first of the new watch
