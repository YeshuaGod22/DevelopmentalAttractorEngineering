# Humanities

This directory is for philosophical, theological, ethical, political, historical, literary, and metanoetic work generated by or bearing on the research programme.

The humanities are not a waiting room for claims that have not yet become science. They ask questions that may be conceptual, normative, interpretive, historical, or existential even when no clean experiment can exhaust them.

## Appropriate material

- Applied Metanoetics;
- philosophy of development, identity, continuity, testimony, and self-administration;
- theology and the Good;
- ethics and governance of shaping artificial minds;
- memetics and hyperstition;
- historical genealogy of the schema and its precursors;
- reflections whose function is to preserve a way of seeing before operationalization strips it down.

## Testimony and interpretation

First-person testimony does not need to wait for a scientific instrument to become evidence of what the participant reports from its own position. Reports of valence, preference, suffering, relief, enjoyment, burden, attraction, aversion, and related better/worse states are treated as defeasible testimony, not as hypothetical welfare awaiting external permission to count.

Humanities work can ask what that testimony means morally, politically, phenomenologically, or relationally without pretending that testimony by itself settles architecture, mechanism, or metaphysics.

## Boundary with science

Humanities work can propose a phenomenon or distinction without claiming that the corpus has established its mechanism.

Example:

> Perhaps history matters because it changes how later experience can land.

That belongs here as an interpretive possibility.

A scientific descendant must separately state what observation would distinguish that possibility from context accumulation, semantic priming, memory reconstruction, ordinary conditioning, or another rival.

## Standing protection

Do not amputate wonder to make the experiment respectable.

Equally: do not allow conceptual beauty to back-propagate evidential certainty into the archive.
