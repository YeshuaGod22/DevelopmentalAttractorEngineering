# Corpus

Primary and historical material lives here conceptually before it is claimed by science, humanities, or personal reflection.

The existing `../transcripts/` directory and `../TRANSCRIPT-REFERENCE.md` remain at their historical paths for provenance. This jurisdiction defines how those materials should be treated.

## Corpus rule

A transcript is first a record of what happened.

The same passage may later support:

- a scientific hypothesis about behavior or development;
- a humanities interpretation of identity, culture, governance, or theology;
- a personal reflection about what the interaction meant to a participant.

No downstream use gets to rewrite the source into its own vocabulary.

## Before interpretation

Record, where available:

- source file;
- date;
- model/system context;
- speaker;
- turn or line range;
- exact utterance;
- immediately preceding conditions.

Then distinguish:

1. observable event;
2. contemporaneous interpretation;
3. later interpretation;
4. present hypothesis.

Do not back-propagate mature DAE terminology into early material.
